**Verdict** — Stop early?
Stop early. The story becomes incoherent at Turn 51, but severe readability issues with placeholders, pronoun ambiguity, and plot looping make it a frustrating read long before that point.

**Book score** — 1–10 for standalone story quality
4/10. The story has a strong premise and moments of excellent atmospheric prose, but it is critically undermined by placeholder text, severe pronoun ambiguity in action scenes, and a repetitive plot that circles the same locations and encounters without meaningful progression.

**Free hook** — YES / MAYBE / NO a Free player comes back tomorrow
MAYBE. The first 12 turns are a strong hook, establishing stakes and ending on a tense cliffhanger with a competent antagonist, and a durable delta (combat initiated, escape failed) is landed. However, the subsequent turns (13-20) become a confusing muddle of fighting and talking that fails to capitalize on the initial momentum, which might cause a player to lose interest.

**YES/NO gates** — the five shared craft gates

- **No unrequested recycle:** NO. The player returns to the fence multiple times (e.g., T29, T32) and is ambushed by different but functionally identical groups of thugs repeatedly (T9, T34, T45).
- **Turn delta exists (or honest exhaustion):** NO. Turns 22, 23, 30, and 33 involve inspecting objects with no new information or consequences, stalling the plot. The loops with the fence also lack meaningful state change between them.
- **Distinct choice outcomes:** NO. The player repeatedly chooses to walk away from the fence, only to immediately return in a subsequent turn, suggesting the outcomes are not meaningfully branching the narrative.

**Best stretch** — 1–3 turns that worked as fiction

- **Turn 12:** The Pact-Hunter effortlessly cutting off the player's escape attempt is a great beat that establishes him as a competent and dangerous antagonist.
- **Turn 8:** The reveal that the summoning pact binds the summoner, not the summoned, is a fantastic narrative twist that immediately grants the player a strong sense of agency and leverage.
