1.  **Verdict** — Stop early? I would stop reading at Turn 10, where the story abruptly and inexplicably teleports the character from a collapsing vault to a snowy street fight, shattering all narrative continuity.

2.  **Book score** — 3/10. While individual paragraphs contain strong prose and the initial premise is excellent, the complete collapse of causality and continuity after the first act makes it an incoherent and frustrating read.

3.  **Free hook** — MAYBE. The first 8 turns are a fantastic hook that would absolutely bring a player back, but the story completely falls apart at Turn 10, well within the first free session, which would likely cause a confused player to quit. T12 does show a durable delta (a fight is joined and state is changed), but the subsequent turns immediately contradict and reset this progress, nullifying the hook.
