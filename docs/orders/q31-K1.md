# q31-K1: commitments from the turn record (Order K part 1)

Tag `q31-K1`. Stamp `2026-10-16z1`. Play change: the scene ledger's pinned promises are engine commitments, so the writer packet changes. Edge `types.ts` and `npcRelationships.ts` changed, so `gm-turn` is redeployed.

Tabletop and shared paths only. PYOA flag (plan item 5) skipped.

## Cause

`src/game/sceneLedger.ts` `stampSceneLedger` (the non-threat branch, formerly ~193) pinned every campaign-memory `Open thread` that was not a threat. Those lines were written by `extractPromisesFromProse` (`campaignMemory.ts:146`, `PROMISE_PATTERNS`). `commitTurnRecord` (`stateTx.ts`) never recorded a promise. `PromiseRecord` (`npcRelationships.ts:50`) had no kind, parties, or engine condition, and `WorldDeal` had no `commitmentId`.

## Fix

One record: `Commitment` in `types.ts` (widened `PromiseRecord`: kind promise/deal/agreement, by, to, condition, checkedTurn; status adds expired). `export type PromiseRecord = Commitment` in `npcRelationships.ts` and the edge copy. `WorldDeal.commitmentId`. `GameState.commitments`.

`recordTurnCommitments` (`stateTx.ts`) runs inside `commitTurnRecord`, which both play paths already share. A `quest_reveal` row for a quest with a giver becomes a promise to that giver, condition `quest-complete`. An active world deal with no id yet becomes a deal with its partner, condition `deal-runs`, and the deal points at that id. No engine target means no commitment and no condition. Prose is not read.

Order M: pinned ledger promises are the open commitments for people here and in Previous (`openCommitmentsFor`). The Open-thread promise branch is cut (logged). `extractPromisesFromProse` still writes Open-thread lines for threats and memory; it no longer feeds promises. Its patterns are frozen, not deleted, because that call is still used.
