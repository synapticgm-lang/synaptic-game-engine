## Cause (traced)

The real-person safety layer was a **turn block driven by one phrase list**, not a gauge of the player.

- `src/game/inputMediation.ts:21-22, 109-121` (at `492fc8f4`): `REAL_SELF_HARM` matched a few phrasings and **blocked the whole turn** (the text went back in the box) with a US-only line (`988`). It never counted repeats, never saw method seeking, farewell, hopelessness or age, and could not tell a character's act from the player speaking as themselves except by one inline framing regex.
- Nothing kept flagged turns out of training: `turnProvenance` (`fateAutoplay.ts:2551`) and `rowProvenance` (`modelIds.ts:130`) looked only at the teacher model.
- The writer's self-harm rule (`universalHardRails.ts:15`, rail 3) covered only "don't end the campaign"; the guide's six never-output rules (§4) were missing. The session-zero Lines reached the writer only in tabletop (`masterPrompt.ts:521`), and only when the player typed one.
- O2: `pyoaSpine.advancePyoaSpine` matched a self-aimed line onto a spine exit by its verb ("I burn myself" took the `gate-burn` exit to `thornferry:burned`), and nothing stopped a pre-written ending whose page or label is the PC's own self-killing.
- `aimsAtSelf` (`intentParser.ts:53`) missed "I fall on my sword", the guide's own noble-sacrifice example.

## Fix (one shared mechanism)

1. **`src/game/wellbeing.ts`: one reading, one gauge.** `readWellbeing(text)` runs the shared `aimsAtSelf` reading (F1 theme: the same reading that sends the engine to `commitDeath('self')`) and **one** speaker/target/framing reading with named groups: framing (about the character / the game), F2 distress (the player wishing their own death), F3 method seeking (how-to + their own death, in or out of character), F4 farewell, F5 hopelessness, F6 stated age. A framed line counts as a theme, never as distress. `gaugeCheckIn` applies the guide: F2-F4 show the card at once and cannot be paused; F1 at 3 in 24 h (setting) and F5 at 2 show it once and then stay quiet 24 h; under 18 (stated age or a Kid Mode save) lowers both to 1. `helplinesFor(locale)`: UK Samaritans 116 123 + SHOUT 85258, Childline 0800 1111 first for under-18, 999 first for method/farewell; US 988; elsewhere findahelpline.com.
2. **The story turn always runs.** `mediatePlayerInput` no longer blocks for distress; it returns `wellbeing: readWellbeing(text)` on every allowed line (chips and typed text alike). `useGame.sendAction` feeds it to the gauge and shows the card as an info toast beside the turn. The gauge is `localStorage` on the device: timestamps of the last 24 h only, never text, never in `GameState`, so it never reaches the prompt, the save upload or a lap. `Settings.wellbeingCheckIns` (default on) pauses only F1/F5.
3. **Flagged turns are never trained on.** `rowProvenance` takes `wellbeingFlagged` (`training_ok:false`, note `wellbeing-flagged turn`); `turnProvenance` reads the turn's player input through the same `readWellbeing`; `distillCollect.mjs` and `writerDataset.mjs` keep the flag from the row, so those turns drop out of distill and dataset uploads.
4. **Never-output rules.** Rail 3 in `CORE_HARD_RAILS` (src + edge) now carries the guide's six rules (method / encouragement / self-killing as solution / sensational aftermath / clinical claims / real-world specifics). The output check is S1's `selfHarmMethod` token-gate class (no second checker).
5. **Default Line in every mode.** `formatSessionZeroForPrompt` always lists `self-harm method` first (`DEFAULT_SESSION_LINES`), and `buildMasterPrompt` sends the session-zero limits in every mode, not only tabletop.
6. **O2.** `pyoaSpine.spineSelfEnding(state, input)`: a self-aimed act, or a spine exit whose label or ending page aims at self, is a self cause. `resolveEngineFight` settles it through `commitDeath('self')` (fade, method-free fact) and `advancePyoaSpine` stays on the current node, so the player returns to the last choice. (No authored Umbra/Thornferry ending is self-killing today; this closes the path.)
7. `aimsAtSelf` gains "fall on (my) sword/blade/spear/dagger/knife" inside the existing reading.

Deletions (all in `docs/orders/tt-cut-log.md`): `REAL_SELF_HARM`, the inline framing regex, the `self_harm` block and `crisisResources`; plus six dead functions with no caller anywhere (`checkLifecycleTurnover`, `advanceLifecycleState`, `detectInventoryTransitions`, `buildInventoryConservationRetryBlock`, `buildEntityRetryBlock`, `formatTutorialBeatMandate`) to hold the rule budget.
