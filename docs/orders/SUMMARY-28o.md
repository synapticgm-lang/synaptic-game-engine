Auto Fight picks the approach with the best expected result and puts it in the template and STATUS.
- Social, game-wide: new socialLeverage.ts adds named modifiers to the one engine social check (runPlayerCheck): +2 for naming a person, place or quest learned in play; +1/+2 for showing a carried item or offering coin you have; worn gear vs the target's role class, where each gear trait and role is a class, not a story list (holy +2 / unholy -3 with clergy, badge +2 / criminal -2 with guards, fine +2 / ragged -2 with nobles, and so on; worn total capped at +/-4); and for intimidation, STR over CHA, martial gear, and trained intimidation/deception skills.
