The two Order F cases still failing had different causes.

## Cause 1: the fight's light line names and counts the foe differently from the fight log

- `src/game/tabletop/roundFight.ts:281-289` (`fightLightNote`) named the foe by the road label (`enc.name`, "the restless dead") and took the headcount from the encounter (`enc.count`). The fight it had just started already has its own foes (`started.combat.foes`), cut to one zombie by tt-fix-2's SRD budget. q31-A logs a lone SRD foe by its stat-block name ("Zombie"). The STATUS line therefore read "the restless dead **sees** by Darkvision" beside "Zombie Slam" in the round line.
- **Fix:** `fightLightNote` takes the started fight's setup. It counts the fight's foes and names a lone foe through the shared `foeIdentity(...).statusName` (Order A's helper); a group keeps the encounter label. No new naming code. Bandits (several) are unchanged: "the thugs are Blinded".
- **Test expectation changed (cited):** `tt6a.bank.ts` T13 dark fight now expects "Zombie sees by Darkvision" and "Zombie Slam" (one zombie since tt-fix-2's SRD budget; the log name since q31-A).

## Cause 2: a span the clock recorded was read as "the travel record read aloud"

- `src/game/engineFactPatterns.ts:19-21, 36-37` (tt-fix-3): whenever the turn's engine fact held a clock or travel record, **any** narrated span ("half an hour", "two hours") counted as `RECORD_READOUT`, so the sentence repair cut the line. On s83 T8 the record says "+30 min, now midday" and the writer wrote "Jax walked for half an hour…". That is the clock told as story, which is what the order asks for ("half an hour comes from the clock record"). The line was cut at `finishCommittedProse`.
- **Fix:** deleted that branch, along with `NUM` and `DURATION`. Record readouts stay owned by the one shared `TRAVEL_RECORD` list (legs, "the journey took…", "travelled for N hours", "part of the travel"), which the game gate and `scripts/factCheck.mjs` both import. Every pinned readout test (ttFix2, ttFix3) is leg or trip wording and still flags. The cut is logged in `tt-cut-log.md`.
- The time-of-day check is unchanged: the "morning light" line at midday is still dropped (`place:time`).
