# q33-N3c — TT38-N3c fallback page from the turn record

## Cause

`lastResortStoryBody` (`src/game/completedEventPacket.ts`, the travel-only read before the pool) painted a page from `travelBeatFact` only. A dungeon, search, talk, or rest turn has no travel beat, so it fell through to `topicAdvancePool`, which follows the player's words. The arrival branch then built `behindLine` from `facts.present` and from `leftFrom`. `leftFrom` can be the underway label `UNDERWAY_HERE` (`src/game/locationName.ts` line 31, "On the road between places"). `stayedPreposition` adds "on", which is the lap 38 C T16 page: "stayed on On the road between places", with the inn's people, and "reached" when the record had not moved.

## Fix

`writerEngineResult` (`src/game/travelJourney.ts`, and the edge stub) is the one receipt-to-sentence form. Travel receipts stay as they were. A `Dungeon room: X, inside Y` receipt becomes "You stood in X inside Y." `Ways on:` becomes "A way led on to …" (the parenthetical dropped). A `Rest:` receipt becomes the rest, including HP when the receipt states a change. `lastResortStoryBody` renders this turn's travel beat and `completedEvent.engineResult` through that form before the pool. The pool runs only when that record is empty.

A left-behind sentence names `leftBehind` only, and only when `leftFrom` is a mapped place (`pagePlace`: not `isRoadLabel`, not a trip HERE). The present list is not used.
