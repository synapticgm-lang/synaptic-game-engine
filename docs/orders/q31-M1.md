# q31-M1: scene ledger on the place (Order M part 1, steps 1–2)

Tag `q31-M1`. Stamp `2026-10-16r`. Play change: each committed turn writes the ledger onto the current place, so the stamp bumps and the client ships. Edge `types.ts` changed, so `gm-turn` is redeployed. Steps 3–6 are not this order.

## Cause

`src/game/infoSheet.ts:34` (`LAST_CAP = 3`) and `lastLines` at `:127` keep three turns. `PlaceRecord` (`src/game/types.ts`, the `dungeonCard` field) stored the dungeon card and nothing else about the scene. Anything said or seen earlier in the same place had nowhere to live. The dungeon card (`src/game/dungeonCard.ts`, `dungeonRoomFacts`) was the pattern: one engine record on the place, updated from engine flags.

## Fix

One ledger on `PlaceRecord.sceneLedger` (`SceneLedger` in `types.ts`). `stampSceneLedger` (`src/game/sceneLedger.ts`) runs at the live commit (`useGame.ts`, after `commitTurnRecord`) and the Fate commit (`fateAutoplay.ts`, same point).

It only reads records the engine already wrote:

- `said` from `NpcMemory.said` (filled by `recordSpokenTopics`) for people here
- `promises` / `threats` / `asked` from unresolved campaign-memory threads (`extractPromisesFromProse` / `pinOpenPlayerAsk` already write those threads)
- `present` from `sceneFacts.present` and `presentNpcRecords`
- `spot` from `activeHereSpot`
- `described` from the accepted beat's sentences (`splitProseSentences`), with `handedRefs` masked out of the key, deduped, oldest dropped past 24

In a dungeon, the current card room's live foes join `present` and revealed secrets join `clues`. `dungeonCard` stays the room store. The stamp does not call `closeDungeon`.

No writer block, no repeat gate, no scene-exit condense. Those are later parts.
