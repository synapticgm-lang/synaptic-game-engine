## Cause

tt-build-roadmeet made a road meeting an ENGINE RESULT event, never a person present. Two things still found the meeting by text or by a field it may not have:

1. **Chips.** A road chip was recognised by label, not by the live meeting.
   - `travelJourney.ts:545` (at `bb3a86be`) `isJourneyPad` matched a static list of every kind's pads, live meeting or not.
   - `choiceWarden.ts:174/183` (at `bb3a86be`) kept a road chip only on an exact label match with `journeyPads(state)`. The live thugs pad carries the failure-cost tail ("…, but fail and they attack"), so "Talk your way past the thugs" (s79 T14) fell through to the invented-context test and was dropped because the prose says "three rough men".
2. **Talk answers.** `npcSheet.ts:246` `motiveLinesOf` reached the thugs/camp group lines only through `roadMeetingWant`, which returned '' when the meeting had no recorded `want` (every 12-13 Oct tape). So the thugs (m39 fl188 T20) had "nothing on record" and the answer repair never asked them.

## Fix (one source: the live meeting's class)

- `travelJourney.ts:549` `isJourneyPad(state, choice)`: walk on / turn back / travel engine chip, or one of the live meeting's own pads (`encounterPads`, by its class), compared through `meetingMoveBase` (tail or no tail). A meeting chip with no such meeting on the stretch is no longer a road chip, so it goes through the normal listener check.
- `choiceWarden.ts:183`: road chips are kept through `isJourneyPad(state, choice)` instead of exact-label membership (same reading as `chipLegality.ts:193`).
- `travelJourney.ts:932` `roadMeetingSpeaker` returns the meeting record a speaker label or name stands for (by class). `npcSheet.ts:246` takes group lines from its kind and uses `want` only for the non-group "I want …" line.
- No person is added to presence, and there's no new checker or word list. Three cuts are logged in `tt-cut-log.md`.

Camps keep "Approach the camp" (which keeps the meeting open to talk) plus "Face the camp's champion" when one is there. Pleas keep "Help them". No new pads were authored.
