# Confirmed Research Target List

This is the complete independent research list for the public-competitive portion of the audit. Each target will be researched against public first-party product, help, pricing, safety, and policy pages where available, with public reviews used only as clearly labeled supplementary evidence.

| ID | Target | Why it is in scope | Primary questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | AI Dungeon | Major AI text-adventure / AI GM reference product. | Memory, story cards, model access, images, subscriptions, safety, continuity controls. |
| R2 | Friends & Fables | Direct AI tabletop-GM competitor. | Campaign structure, character sheet/inventory, dice/combat, multiplayer implications, pricing, memory claims. |
| R3 | Hidden Door | Narrative social roleplay competitor. | Canon/licensed-world posture, collaborative storytelling, content mediation, accessibility, monetization. |
| R4 | Summon Worlds | AI RPG / worldbuilding competitor. | Campaign persistence, character and world data, AI storytelling, pricing, platform positioning. |
| R5 | NovelAI | Adventure-adjacent AI-writing product. | Lorebook/memory mechanisms, image feature economics, privacy framing, subscription. |
| R6 | Strong tabletop AI GM: Quest Portal | Public tabletop AI GM reference. | Campaign setup, rules support, persistent play, subscriptions, trust controls. |
| R7 | Strong tabletop AI GM: RPG Stories | Public AI RPG / tabletop reference. | Game-master behavior, character progression, platform/pricing, persistence claims. |
| R8 | Trust, children, privacy, ads, refunds | Cross-cutting launch/compliance research. | COPPA/UK child design, privacy expectations, FTC advertising/refund signals, app-ad constraints. |
| R9 | Cost and model-service public benchmarks | Boundary evidence for E5 framework. | Public model/API pricing and cost-observability practices; no fabricated SynapticGM inputs. |

## Research Method

The audit will favor **first-party public pages**. Every operational claim will include a URL and 2026-08-18 access date. Where a claim cannot be verified from public material, the audit will say `UNVERIFIED`, describe the test needed, and avoid asserting internal implementation details.
