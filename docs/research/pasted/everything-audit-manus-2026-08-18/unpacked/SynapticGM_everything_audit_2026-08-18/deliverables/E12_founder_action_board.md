## What to Ask Counsel This Week

Ask for decisions, not generic reassurance: target age/audience and Kid Mode posture; data map/retention/deletion/export for messages and ledger revisions; Google/Stripe/analytics/ad/SDK flows; privacy/terms/refund/subscription language; UGC/IP/moderation/takedown; claim substantiation; and incident/notification expectations. Provide the counsel packet before the meeting.

## Founder Decision Cadence

| Meeting | Inputs | Required decision |
|---|---|---|
| Daily 15 minutes | New P0s, CostEvent anomalies, failed CI fixtures. | Fix, disable, or explicitly defer; no silent acceptance. |
| Weekly 45 minutes | E4 quotes, trace diffs, cost/latency tails, safety labels, support issues. | Choose one dominant failure; prevent backlog sprawl. |
| Monthly 90 minutes | Cohort economics, conversion/refunds, counsel decisions, claim register, incident drills. | Set/adjust fair limits and beta scope; decide whether GPU gate evidence exists. |

The 90-day outcome is not “bigger.” It is a narrow product whose causal-chain promise is evidenced, explainable, safe to operate, and economically measured.
