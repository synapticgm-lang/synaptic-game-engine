## 0. PRODUCT LAW (non-negotiable)

1. Art pixels ≠ lettering.
 - Live turns: image model renders PURE SCENE art only (no dialogue, captions, SFX text, logos, UI).

 - Ban: “make it like Marvel / DC / [manga title] / [living artist name]”.
 - Allow: original technique shapes (ink wash, screentone grain, high-contrast silhouette, cel shade, vertical scroll gutters) described without franchise names.

5. Story truth is the text/ledger. Art never invents NPCs, props, outcomes, wounds, or places. Art failure must not block play.

6. Overlay lettering stays (prior founder decision). Do not reopen “bake bubbles into Flux” for live turns.
