## 10 Hero Awakening-shaped opening replies

1. A brass token wakes warm in your palm just as the ferry rope snaps. The river takes the boat’s lantern first.
2. The warning bell rings once beneath the market, though no bell hangs there. A seam of blue light runs between the cobbles.
3. You wake with ash on your sleeve and a map drawn beneath your skin in fading ink. Outside, someone is calling for the person who survived the fire.
4. At dawn, every mirror in the boarding house shows the same locked door. Yours is the only room with a key.
5. The quarry shifts before your first step lands. A voice below the stone says your name as if it has been practicing.
6. Your borrowed blade hums when the magistrate lies. Tonight, she has summoned you to dinner.
7. A courier leaves a sealed glass vial at your door. Inside it, a tiny storm circles a paper bearing your signature.
8. The old well gives back the coin you threw into it years ago—along with a second coin minted tomorrow.
9. The city’s ward-lamps go out in a single breath. One remains lit above the alley where your friend vanished.
10. A ledger appears in your pack with one line already written: **Debt paid by sunrise.**

## 10 Blank Canvas-shaped opening replies

1. Rain darkens the station platform. A porter holds up a card with a name that is not yours, then looks straight at you.
2. The rented room contains one chair, one cracked window, and a letter addressed to whoever arrives first.
3. In the market’s last hour, a child offers you a key and asks whether you remember the door it fits.
4. The village has put a chair in the road. No one will say who is meant to sit in it.
5. A narrow boat waits beneath the bridge, tied badly enough that the current keeps testing it.
6. The innkeeper has saved a table by the stove. The place card bears your handwriting, though you have never been here.
7. A dog trots out of the fog with a red ribbon tied to its collar and a library stamp on the ribbon’s tag.
8. At the archive desk, a clerk pushes forward an empty file marked **URGENT** and waits for you to speak first.
9. The train stops between towns. Beyond the glass, a single lantern moves across the field toward you.
10. A stranger at the harbor mistakes you for someone they have been waiting to forgive.

## Bank usage guards

Use a skeleton only after adjudication. Treat placeholder content as a render slot, not a factual assertion. Do not reuse an opening construction within the same campaign. Do not deploy a repair line without naming the actual contrast. Do not turn a System notice into a replacement for scene body.
