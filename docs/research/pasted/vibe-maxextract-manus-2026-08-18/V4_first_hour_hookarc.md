# V4 — First Hour and HookArc Feel

## First-hour principle

Onboarding is not a lore dump or a tutorial overlay. It is the player’s first proof that the core loop works: **notice → speak/act freely → resolve fairly → observe durable change**. Early learning is constrained by attention and working memory, so each turn should introduce one new commitment while retaining a stable response shape. [1] Contextual action before explanation is generally more effective than teaching mechanics in isolation. [2]

Honeymoon turns are not merely free quota. They are a short period of unusually **high recognition density**: clear personal response, rapid consequence, an earned callback, and a visible promise that the campaign will stay coherent.

## Beat sheet A — “Threshold Event” (System-integration-shaped; original)

| Turn | Player experience | Runtime / ledger behavior | New verb taught |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ordinary action interrupted by a world anomaly with an immediate personal stake. | Create SceneManifest; introduce one non-authoritative System notice. | Observe |
| 2 | Player states a natural-language response. | IntentContract resolves a low-risk action; no menu required. | Speak / act |
| 3 | The world answers with a small, legible change. | StateTx: `knowledge +1` or `position changed`; show short receipt. | Inspect |
| 4 | A nearby character reacts differently to the player’s approach. | Relationship/evidence distinction shown in fiction. | Address |
| 5 | A first rule appears only because the player uses it. | Permit-gated mechanic introduced after safe use. | Attempt |
| 6 | A low-stakes resistance exposes a limit without shaming. | Fair failure: condition and aftermath readable; no reset. | Adapt |
| 7 | Player can protect, investigate, bargain, or leave. | Typed choice commitments with distinct immediate meanings. | Choose |
| 8 | Consequence closes one easy route and opens a specific pressure. | StateTx/HookArc update; outcome receipt. | Commit |
| 9 | The System acknowledges an earlier phrase or choice without replaying it. | Callback gets a payoff or is suppressed. | Recall |
| 10 | The player sees a personally framed next objective and can continue in freeform. | Scene boundary; optional inspection/recap available now, not mid-action. | Continue |

**Vibe line:** “The rule did not arrive to explain itself. It arrived because you reached for something the old world could not give.”

## Beat sheet B — “Awakening Under Pressure” (hero-awakening-shaped; original)

| Turn | Player experience | Runtime / ledger behavior | New verb taught |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The player is already in a vulnerable situation with a clear human anchor. | Manifest includes threat, ally, exit, and uncertainty. | Orient |
| 2 | Player chooses a personal response rather than a build/class. | IntentContract holds exact language; no premature taxonomy. | Declare |
| 3 | An unexpected capacity manifests with a narrow, explainable effect. | Create permitted ability state only if CampaignContract allows. | Use |
| 4 | The ability has a cost, uncertainty, or social consequence. | StateTx records cost; System explains only what is observable. | Weigh |
| 5 | NPC response tests whether player wants power, safety, truth, or connection. | Relationship branch persists even if plot reconverges. | Answer |
| 6 | A first challenge is telegraphed in prose and chrome. | Check card or combat cue explains stakes. | Risk |
| 7 | Player succeeds or fails with visible cause. | OutcomeToken and state delta; no hidden substitution. | Recover |
| 8 | A choice reveals differing values, not merely different rewards. | Commitment tags feed HookArc. | Define |
| 9 | A brief memorable splash can celebrate a real discovery/change. | Event-driven splash only; no every-turn comic. | Mark |
| 10 | The opening promise names the future pressure created by the player’s choice. | HookArc opens / branches; optional campaign UI at safe boundary. | Pursue |

**Vibe line:** “It was not a destiny. It was a capability—and now people were going to notice what you did with it.”

## Teaching by action

| Teach only after | Surface | Do not do |
|---|---|---|
| First safe freeform action | Intent echo in the result. | Explain all command syntax. |
| First uncertain attempt | Compact check or outcome card. | Present a rules handbook. |
| First state change | Receipt plus inspect affordance. | Dump raw ledger fields. |
| First social consequence | Character reaction and relationship shift. | Declare hidden affinity numbers as canon. |
| First commitment | A scoped promise/quest card. | Add five unrelated objectives. |
| First failure | Cause, state, next viable action. | Reset or scold the player. |

## First-hour anti-patterns

**System-only turns** detach the game from lived stakes. **Recycled collage** gives the impression of model sampling rather than a continuous world. **Invented NPC biography** feels personal until it collides with later canon. **Ignored questions** prove that the game is generating at the player rather than playing with them. **Early configuration overload** converts genre anticipation into form completion.

## Instrumentation

Measure time to first meaningful action, first legible consequence, first correction success, first choice whose result a player can explain, and voluntary turn 11. Ask: “What changed because of you?”, “What is true now?”, and “What can you try next?” Narrative comprehension and narrative appeal are distinct; test both. [3]

**SPECULATIVE:** These turn counts are a purposeful prototype, not a universal retention formula. Run moderated novice, expert, and Kid Mode sessions.  
**COUNSEL:** Opening-pressure intensity and first-hour content taxonomy require safety policy review.
