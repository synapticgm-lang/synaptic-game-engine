# Public-Domain Corpus Verification

Project Gutenberg describes itself as a library of free ebooks focused on older works whose U.S. copyright has expired. Its catalogue visibly includes **The Odyssey**, **The Mysteries of Udolpho**, and **The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes**, supporting technique-level references for mythic, gothic, and case-file narration. Source: [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/).

Standard Ebooks provides a browsable catalogue with subject filters including **Adventure, Children’s, Comedy, Fantasy, Horror, Mystery, Satire, Science Fiction,** and **Shorts**. The site states that content produced by or for Standard Ebooks is dedicated to the public domain under CC0, while warning that other displayed content may carry separate rights. Source: [Browse Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks).

## Use restriction for this project

These collections justify using public-domain works as **technique exemplars**, not copying source passages into product-facing banks. The implementation pack will summarize structural devices—such as invocation, cataloguing, epistolary fragments, clue-led observation, domestic scene compression, and objective-correlative atmosphere—without issuing imitation instructions. Public-domain status in the United States does not establish worldwide freedom; releases in additional jurisdictions should receive **COUNSEL** review.
