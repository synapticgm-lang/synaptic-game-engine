# Opener Pointer Examples

**Status:** **ORIGINAL SynapticGM** writer-training material.  
**Audience:** Internal writer and prompt-bank maintainers. These are page-one examples, not canned player cards and not UI chips.

> **How to use:** Treat each `SNAPSHOT FACTS` line as binding. Treat each `FLAIR` line as expendable sensory direction. A writer may vary the flair, rhythm, and imagery, but must not turn flair into new inventory, companions, ranks, exits, weather, time passage, or accepted objectives.

## Shared Rules

| Rule | Application |
| --- | --- |
| Viewpoint | Adult viewpoint in all examples; use a child viewpoint only when a real player biography explicitly says the character is a child. |
| Present cast | Only people named in `SNAPSHOT FACTS` may act or speak. |
| Objects | Only listed carried or visible objects may be put in hand or treated as available. |
| Pressure | End with a specific choice-pressure question that leaves agency intact. |
| Originality | Use generic folklore, original speculative systems, and ordinary architecture; never inject licensed titles, named styles, or copied world elements. |
| Provenance | Rumour stays rumour, offer stays open, and a task becomes accepted only through explicit player commitment. |
| Continuity | Time, weather, indoor/outdoor state, exits, crowd scale, and companions do not change merely for drama. |


## Isekai Summon

The "isekai summon" family drops the viewpoint character abruptly into a new, often magical world via a deliberate ritual or accidental tear in reality. These openings establish immediate disorientation, foreign surroundings, and the pressing expectations of whoever—or whatever—brought them there.

### The Chalk Circle
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, stone dungeon, robed summoner, glowing chalk circle, midnight, trapped inside the circle.
**FLAIR:** ozone scent, chanting echoes, flickering torches.

The blinding flash of light fades, leaving the harsh scent of ozone burning in your nostrils. You are no longer standing in your kitchen. Instead, cold stone presses against your bare feet, and flickering torchlight dances across a damp dungeon wall. You are standing perfectly centered within a complex, glowing chalk circle. Just outside its boundary stands a figure draped in heavy, moth-eaten robes, their face hidden beneath a deep cowl. The chanting that accompanied your arrival slows to a halt, and the robed figure leans heavily on a wooden staff, panting. They raise a trembling hand toward you, fingers curled in a mixture of awe and desperation. The magical barrier of the circle hums with static electricity, keeping you confined. What do you do as the stranger takes a hesitant step forward?

### The Crystal Pedestal
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, grand marble hall, crystal pedestal, shattered glass, midday sunlight, surrounded by armored guards.
**FLAIR:** chiming shards, polished armor, tense silence.

Gravity reasserts itself with a sickening lurch, and you crash hard onto a smooth, elevated surface. You push yourself up, finding yourself kneeling on a massive crystal pedestal in the center of a grand marble hall. Sunlight streams through towering stained-glass windows, illuminating the thousands of shattered glass shards scattered around you—the remains of whatever vessel you just burst from. A tense, heavy silence fills the cavernous room. Surrounding the base of the pedestal is a tight ring of armored guards, their spears leveled directly at your chest. Their polished breastplates reflect the bright midday sun, and their eyes are wide with shock beneath their visors. Nobody moves, but the spears do not waver. How do you react to the ring of steel pointed at you?

### The Forest Clearing
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, dense pine forest, frightened villager, iron sword, dusk, cornered by wolves.
**FLAIR:** snapping twigs, pine needles, low growls.

One moment you are walking down a city street, and the next, you are plunging through thick branches to land heavily on a bed of pine needles. The air is cool and smells sharply of sap and damp earth. The dim light of dusk filters through the dense canopy above. A few feet away, a frightened villager in homespun clothes is backed against a massive tree trunk, clutching a rusty iron sword with trembling hands. A low growl draws your attention to the brush. Three massive, silver-furred wolves are pacing at the edge of the clearing, their yellow eyes locked on the villager—and now, on you. The snapping of twigs under their paws breaks the tense quiet as they begin to close the circle. What is your next move as the pack advances?

### The Summoning Sickness
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, moving wooden wagon, elderly healer, clay cup, heavy rain, intense nausea.
**FLAIR:** rattling wheels, bitter herbs, drumming rain.

You wake to the violent lurching of a moving wooden wagon and the deafening drum of heavy rain against a canvas roof. A wave of intense nausea rolls through your stomach, making the cramped space spin. You are lying on a pile of rough burlap sacks. Sitting across from you is an elderly healer with a weathered face, wearing a patched woolen cloak. She watches you intently, holding out a small, cracked clay cup filled with a steaming, dark liquid that smells strongly of bitter herbs. The wagon hits a deep rut, rattling your bones and nearly spilling the drink. The healer gestures for you to take the cup, her expression grave and urgent, as the storm rages outside. How do you respond to the offered remedy?

### The Altar of Sand
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, desert ruin, kneeling priest, stone altar, scorching sun, bound wrists.
**FLAIR:** blowing sand, heat shimmer, dry wind.

The sensation of falling ends abruptly as you slam onto a sun-baked stone altar. The scorching midday heat immediately blisters your skin, and the dry wind whips coarse sand across your face. You try to push yourself up, but a thick, coarse rope binds your wrists tightly together behind your back. You are in the center of a crumbling desert ruin, surrounded by endless dunes that ripple in the heat shimmer. Kneeling in the sand at the base of the altar is a lone priest adorned in brass jewelry and faded linen. He presses his forehead to the ground, murmuring a frantic prayer in a language you do not understand. He has not yet looked up to see you struggling against your bindings. What do you do as the heat beats down on you?

### The Throne Room Arrival
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, opulent throne room, young monarch, velvet carpet, evening, uninvited intrusion.
**FLAIR:** glittering chandeliers, shocked gasps, silk drapery.

A tear in the fabric of space spits you out, and you tumble ungracefully onto a plush red velvet carpet. You scramble to your feet, brushing yourself off, only to realize you are standing in the middle of an opulent throne room. Glittering chandeliers cast a warm evening glow over the silk drapery and gilded columns. At the far end of the hall sits a young monarch wearing a heavy golden crown, their eyes wide with sheer disbelief. A collective gasp ripples through the assembled courtiers lining the walls. You have just interrupted a quiet, formal gathering, and you are entirely uninvited. The monarch slowly rises from the throne, raising a hand to silence the whispering crowd, their gaze fixed squarely on you. How do you address the stunned ruler?

### The Clockwork Laboratory
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, cluttered workshop, eccentric inventor, brass goggles, dawn, entangled in copper wires.
**FLAIR:** ticking gears, escaping steam, metallic tang.

A blinding spark of blue electricity arcs across your vision, and you suddenly find yourself tangled in a massive web of copper wires. The metallic tang of ozone and escaping steam fills the air. You are in a cramped, wildly cluttered workshop, surrounded by spinning brass gears and ticking mechanisms. Pale dawn light filters through a single grime-streaked window. Standing over a smoking control panel is an eccentric inventor with wild hair, wearing thick brass goggles. He stares at you, his jaw slack, as a small fire begins to smolder on the console in front of him. The wires wrapped around your limbs are surprisingly tight, pinning your arms to your sides as the machinery around you continues to whir and click. What is your first action in the chaotic workshop?

### The Ruined Bridge
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, crumbling stone bridge, armored knight, bottomless chasm, howling wind, collapsing stonework.
**FLAIR:** swirling mist, cracking stone, desperate grip.

The portal collapses behind you with a sharp crack, dropping you onto the edge of a crumbling stone bridge. A howling wind immediately tears at your clothes, threatening to push you over the side into the bottomless, mist-filled chasm below. A few paces away, an armored knight is desperately clinging to the cracked stonework, their heavy steel boots slipping on the slick surface. The bridge is actively collapsing; a massive chunk of stone breaks away near your feet and plummets into the abyss. The knight locks eyes with you, reaching out a gauntleted hand as the section of bridge beneath them begins to groan and tilt downward. The wind roars in your ears, making it impossible to hear anything else. What do you do as the bridge crumbles?


## Late Awaken

The "Late Awaken" family drops the player character into a situation already in progress, often waking up or coming to their senses after a gap in consciousness. The immediate constraint usually involves disorientation, a changing environment, or an approaching threat that requires quick assessment.

### The Glass Sarcophagus
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, ruined greenhouse, no other people present, shattered glass, twilight, you are trapped under debris.
**FLAIR:** Scent of crushed orchids, distant howling, cold dampness.

You open your eyes to the sight of jagged glass panes framing a bruised twilight sky. The air is heavy with the scent of crushed orchids and damp earth. You are lying on your back inside what remains of a grand, domed greenhouse, though the metal framework is twisted and rusted. A thick, thorny vine has wrapped itself around your right leg, pinning you beneath a fallen iron strut. The distant howling of something large echoes through the surrounding woods, growing steadily closer. Your head throbs with a dull ache, and your memories of how you arrived in this ruined botanical garden are entirely absent. The thorny vine twitches, tightening its grip slightly against your calf as the howling sounds again. How do you free yourself before the creature reaches the glasshouse?

### The Drifting Skiff
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, wooden rowboat on open water, lone passenger, a single unbroken oar, heavy fog, water is slowly leaking in.
**FLAIR:** Salty mist, creaking wood, eerie silence.

The rhythmic lapping of water against wood slowly pulls you from a deep, dreamless sleep. You are slouched against the stern of a small, wooden rowboat, drifting aimlessly on a glassy, dark sea. A thick, impenetrable fog blankets the water, reducing your visibility to mere feet. The air is biting and thick with salty mist. At your feet lies a single, unbroken wooden oar, along with a rusted metal bucket. A steady drip catches your attention; cold water is slowly seeping through a cracked seam in the floorboards, pooling around your boots. The eerie silence of the fog is broken only by the creaking of the hull and the sloshing of the rising water. The leak is worsening with every passing minute. How do you manage the rising water with only the bucket and the oar?

### The Clockwork Cell
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, circular brass room, an unconscious stranger, ticking gears, midday light through a grate, the walls are slowly contracting.
**FLAIR:** Smell of machine oil, rhythmic clicking, metallic tang.

You gasp for air as consciousness returns, your lungs burning with the sharp scent of machine oil. You are lying on a cold, grated floor inside a perfectly circular room made entirely of tarnished brass. Sunlight streams down from a heavy iron grate high above. Slumped against the opposite wall is an unconscious stranger wearing a tattered gray coat. The walls around you are composed of massive, interlocking gears that tick with a rhythmic, deafening click. With each resounding tick, the brass walls shift inward by a fraction of an inch, slowly contracting the space. You have no tools, and the stranger shows no signs of waking despite the noise. The metallic tang of rust fills the shrinking air. What is your first move to halt the grinding gears before the room crushes you both?

### The Deserted Carriage
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, overturned stagecoach, two panicked horses, scattered luggage, midnight storm, a lantern is starting a fire.
**FLAIR:** Crackling lightning, smell of burning leather, torrential rain.

A deafening crack of thunder jolts you awake. You are suspended upside down, tangled in the velvet curtains of an overturned stagecoach. Outside, torrential rain lashes against the splintered wood, illuminated by flashes of jagged lightning. Two panicked horses are hitched to the broken pole, kicking wildly to break free. Scattered luggage lies in the mud. A shattered oil lantern rests near the seats, its spilled fuel igniting a rapidly growing fire inside the cabin. The smell of burning leather mixes with the storm's ozone. The flames are inching closer to your coat, and the terrified horses threaten to drag the burning coach down the embankment. How do you escape the wreckage while managing the frantic horses?

### The Forgotten Altar
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, subterranean stone chamber, three hooded figures, ceremonial dagger, torchlight, you are bound to a stone slab.
**FLAIR:** Chanting whispers, smell of incense, flickering shadows.

You blink against the harsh glare of flickering torchlight, your vision swimming as you try to focus. You are lying flat on a cold stone slab in the center of a damp, subterranean chamber. Heavy leather straps bind your wrists and ankles to the rock. Surrounding you are three figures draped in deep crimson hoods, their faces hidden in shadow. One of them holds a curved ceremonial dagger, its polished blade reflecting the firelight. The air is thick with the cloying scent of myrrh incense and the low, rhythmic chanting of the hooded figures. The chanting grows louder, and the figure with the dagger steps closer, raising the blade above their head. The leather straps are old and slightly frayed at your left wrist. How do you attempt to break free before the dagger descends?

### The Silent Archive
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, vast library, a hovering mechanical eye, piles of blank parchment, perpetual twilight, a heavy iron collar around your neck.
**FLAIR:** Smell of old dust, humming energy, blue illumination.

You wake slumped over a massive oak desk, your cheek resting on a pile of blank parchment. You are in a vast library where the bookshelves stretch up into a perpetual, glowing blue twilight. A heavy, cold iron collar is locked securely around your neck, emitting a faint, vibrating hum. A few feet away, a mechanical eye encased in a brass sphere hovers silently in the air, its crimson lens fixed directly on you. The smell of ancient dust and ozone fills your lungs. You have no memory of putting on the collar or entering this archive. The hovering eye twitches, emitting a sharp click as it floats a few inches closer, waiting for you to move. How do you investigate the collar and the watcher without triggering an alarm?

### The Frozen Bivouac
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, snow cave, a shivering dog, unlit campfire, howling blizzard, your gloves are missing.
**FLAIR:** Biting wind, numb fingers, scent of pine needles.

The freezing cold snaps you out of your stupor. You are sitting on a bed of pine needles inside a shallow snow cave, shivering violently. Outside, a howling blizzard tears through the mountain pass, driving a wall of white snow past the cave entrance. Huddled against your side is a large, shivering dog with matted fur, whining softly. In front of you is a small pile of dry kindling and an unlit campfire, but your thick winter gloves are nowhere to be found. Your bare fingers are already numb and stiffening from the biting wind that curls into the shelter. Without a fire, the plunging temperature will soon become fatal for both you and the dog. How do you go about lighting the kindling with your freezing, bare hands?

### The Derelict Airlock
**SNAPSHOT FACTS:** Adult viewpoint, metallic airlock chamber, no other people, a flashing red console, emergency sirens, the outer door is slowly opening.
**FLAIR:** Strobe lighting, smell of copper, hissing pressure.

You regain consciousness to the deafening blare of emergency sirens. You are lying on a grated metal floor inside a cramped, hexagonal airlock chamber. Red strobe lights flash rhythmically, casting chaotic shadows across the walls. A control console near the inner hatch is sparking, its screen displaying a flashing warning in a language you cannot read. The sharp smell of copper and ozone burns your nose. With a terrifying hiss of equalizing pressure, the heavy outer door begins to slide open, revealing the absolute blackness of the void beyond. The air is already thinning, pulling at your clothes as the chamber depressurizes. You are not wearing a vacuum suit, and the inner hatch is sealed shut. How do you stop the outer door from opening completely before all the air is sucked out?


## System Apocalypse

System apocalypse openers thrust the player into the exact moment a gamified reality overlays the mundane world. These openings emphasize sudden structural shifts, bizarre interface intrusions into ordinary spaces, and the immediate panic or confusion of the surrounding environment as the old rules of physics and society are instantly rewritten.

### The Office Integration
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult office worker, corporate breakroom, two panicked coworkers, flickering fluorescent lights, buzzing coffee machine, sudden blue holographic screen.
**FLAIR**: ozone smell, sterile corporate colors, ringing in ears.

You are pouring a stale cup of coffee in the breakroom when the fluorescent lights shatter in unison. A heavy, metallic hum vibrates through the floorboards, drowning out the sudden screams from the main floor. Right before your eyes, a glowing blue holographic rectangle materializes in the air, projecting a string of floating text that announces the integration of Earth into the cosmic framework. Your coworkers, Mark and Sarah, are huddled under the main table, weeping as strange geometric patterns etch themselves into the drywall. The coffee machine continues to buzz, leaking scalding water onto the linoleum. The holographic screen chimes, waiting for your acknowledgment while the building groans under an unseen pressure. How do you respond to the glowing prompt hovering in your way?

### Subway Commute
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult commuter, stalled subway car, dozens of confused passengers, flickering emergency lights, hovering selection orb, sealed doors.
**FLAIR**: stale underground air, metallic screeching, neon glow.

The subway lurches to a violent halt, throwing you against the cold plastic seats as the emergency lights flicker on. A deafening metallic screech echoes down the tunnel, followed by an unnatural, echoing voice that declares the tutorial phase has begun. In the center of the crowded car, a glowing golden orb slowly descends from the ceiling, pulsing with an inner neon light. Dozens of passengers press against the sealed doors in panic, their phones displaying only static and strange countdown timers. The orb drifts directly toward you, hovering at eye level and projecting a faint warmth against your face. The air grows stale and heavy, making it difficult to breathe as the countdown on the strangers' phones ticks closer to zero. What do you do as the glowing orb waits for your interaction?

### Grocery Store Shift
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult shopper, grocery store aisle, shattered jars, roaming mechanical hound, evening rain outside, floating inventory interface.
**FLAIR**: smell of spilled vinegar, cold rain, clinical white light.

You stand in the condiment aisle as a violent tremor sends glass jars crashing to the linoleum floor. The evening rain outside is suddenly drowned out by a mechanical howl that reverberates through the grocery store. A sleek, metallic hound with glowing red eyes stalks past the end of the aisle, its claws clicking rhythmically against the tiles. Before you can move, a translucent inventory screen snaps into your field of vision, listing the spilled vinegar and broken glass as harvestable resources. The clinical white lights overhead pulse in time with the mechanical hound’s heavy breathing. It pauses, sniffing the air, its sensors sweeping the aisles for unregistered lifeforms. The floating interface blinks, offering a prompt to equip a nearby broom handle. How do you react as the mechanical beast turns its gaze toward your aisle?

### Highway Traffic Jam
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult driver, gridlocked highway, abandoned cars, descending crystalline pillar, afternoon sun, locked steering wheel.
**FLAIR**: heat waves on asphalt, blinding reflections, low humming noise.

The afternoon sun beats down on the gridlocked highway, but the endless line of cars is completely silent. Your steering wheel locks in place, and the engine dies instantly, joining the graveyard of disabled vehicles stretching to the horizon. From the cloudless sky, a massive crystalline pillar descends, slamming into the asphalt ahead and sending a shockwave that rattles your windows. The heat waves shimmering off the road distort the shape of the pillar, which begins to project floating status bars above the abandoned cars. A low humming noise fills your skull, translating into a robotic voice offering class selection. You are trapped in your seat, the seatbelt jammed, while the crystalline structure emits a wave of pale light that transforms everything it touches. What do you do as the transforming light sweeps toward your vehicle?

### Campus Library
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult student, university library, scattered books, glowing runic circle, ticking clock, locked exit doors.
**FLAIR**: smell of old paper, dust motes, eerie purple illumination.

The quiet hum of the university library is shattered when the heavy wooden exit doors slam shut and lock with a definitive click. The familiar smell of old paper is replaced by the sharp scent of ozone as a glowing runic circle burns itself into the carpet between the study tables. Books tumble from the shelves, their pages fluttering wildly as an unseen wind whips through the room. A translucent purple interface flickers to life in front of you, detailing a localized dungeon manifestation. The large grandfather clock on the wall ticks loudly, its hands spinning backward at an impossible speed. The runic circle pulses, and the faint sound of scratching echoes from beneath the floorboards, growing louder with every passing second. How do you proceed as the purple interface demands you choose a starting weapon?

### Suburban Kitchen
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult homeowner, suburban kitchen, barking dog, floating silver obelisk, morning sunlight, burning toast.
**FLAIR**: smell of charred bread, blinding glare, high-pitched ringing.

The morning sunlight streaming through your kitchen window is abruptly fractured by a floating silver obelisk that phases through the ceiling. Your dog barks frantically at the anomaly, backing into the corner of the room. The toaster pops, but the bread is completely charred, filling the room with acrid smoke. The obelisk projects a blinding glare and a high-pitched ringing that makes your teeth ache. A crisp, floating window appears in your vision, declaring your suburban neighborhood a contested zone and assigning you a level-one status. The walls of your house begin to shimmer, turning briefly transparent to reveal a changed landscape outside, where giant roots are tearing through the street. The interface hovers insistently, flashing a warning about an imminent territorial breach. What do you do as the warning timer begins to count down?

### City Park Jog
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult jogger, city park path, frozen pedestrians, hovering geometric drones, autumn leaves, heavy gravity.
**FLAIR**: crisp autumn air, unnatural silence, metallic clicking.

You are midway through your morning run when the crisp autumn air turns impossibly heavy, forcing you to your knees. Around you, the other pedestrians are completely frozen in place, like statues scattered along the park path. The unnatural silence is broken only by the metallic clicking of geometric drones that descend from the canopy. One of the drones hovers directly in front of you, scanning your face with a sweeping red laser. A transparent window overlays your vision, calculating your physical attributes and offering a mandatory survival tutorial. The gravity continues to press down on your shoulders, making it difficult to stand, while the drone’s laser shifts from red to a welcoming green. The floating window prompts you to accept the new physical parameters of the world. How do you respond to the hovering drone's invitation?

### Hospital Waiting Room
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult visitor, hospital waiting room, unconscious patients, glowing green mist, ticking wall clock, barricaded double doors.
**FLAIR**: antiseptic smell, cold plastic chairs, soft green glow.

The dull murmur of the hospital waiting room falls dead silent as a glowing green mist seeps through the ventilation shafts. The antiseptic smell is overwhelmed by the scent of blooming flora, and within seconds, the other waiting patients slump over in their plastic chairs, completely unconscious. The wall clock ticks loudly, but the second hand is stuck, vibrating in place. A holographic prompt bursts into existence in front of you, declaring the hospital a designated sanctuary zone and offering you the role of caretaker. The barricaded double doors leading to the emergency ward rattle violently as something heavy slams against them from the other side. The soft green glow of the mist begins to crystallize on the floor, forming strange, organic pathways. What do you do as the heavy pounding on the double doors grows louder?


## Tower

The `tower` opener family isolates the protagonist in a high, confined space, creating immediate tension through altitude, isolation, and an urgent localized problem. These scenarios force the player to interact with their immediate environment while dealing with the psychological pressure of being cut off from outside help.

### The Lighthouse Keeper
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult keeper | coastal lighthouse lantern room | alone | brass oil lamp and matches | midnight storm | the main light just went out
**FLAIR**: Smashing waves, smell of ozone, desperate urgency

The storm batters the thick glass of the lantern room with relentless fury, each wave shaking the stone foundation of the lighthouse. You stand alone in the circular chamber, gripping a small brass oil lamp and a box of matches. A moment ago, the massive Fresnel lens was casting its vital beam across the treacherous rocks, but now the main light has abruptly died, plunging the room into darkness. The wind howls through the narrow vents, carrying the sharp scent of ozone and salt. Below, the churning sea threatens any ship caught in its grip. The heavy brass housing of the central burner is still hot to the touch, and the fuel valve feels stiff. You have only minutes before a vessel strikes the reef. How do you attempt to reignite the massive beacon in the pitch black?

### The Observatory Astronomer
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult astronomer | mountain observatory dome | alone | brass telescope and star charts | clear freezing night | a strange object is occluding the moon
**FLAIR**: Piercing cold, silent awe, cosmic scale

The freezing air of the mountain peak bites at your cheeks as you stand in the unheated observatory dome. You are alone with your brass telescope and a table scattered with star charts, the clear night sky offering a perfect view of the heavens. For the past hour, you have been tracking the lunar craters, but something has just shifted. A dark, jagged silhouette is slowly moving across the face of the moon, blocking its pale light. It is far too large to be a bird and moves too deliberately to be a cloud. The silence of the high altitude feels absolute, amplifying the steady beat of your own heart. The telescope's tracking mechanism needs manual adjustment to keep the anomaly in focus before it passes out of view. What do you do to capture the details of this impossible object?

### The Clocktower Mechanic
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult mechanic | interior of a city clocktower | alone | massive bronze gears and heavy wrench | overcast afternoon | the main gear is jammed
**FLAIR**: Smell of grease, rhythmic ticking, vertigo

You balance carefully on the narrow wooden beams high inside the city clocktower, surrounded by the smell of thick grease and old dust. The rhythmic ticking of the smaller escapements echoes around you, but the massive bronze gear that drives the minute hand has ground to a sudden halt. You hold a heavy iron wrench, the only tool you brought up the long spiral stairs this overcast afternoon. Looking down through the gaps in the floorboards induces a wave of vertigo, revealing the dizzying drop to the stone floor below. The great bell is scheduled to strike the hour in just five minutes, and if the jam is not cleared, the tension could shatter the entire mechanism. The jammed gear is wedged tight against a bent iron strut. How do you free the mechanism before the hour strikes?

### The Spire Prisoner
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult prisoner | high stone spire cell | alone | iron bed and wooden bowl | sunset | the door lock just clicked open
**FLAIR**: Dust motes, distant birds, sudden hope

The fading light of sunset filters through the narrow, barred window of your high stone spire cell, illuminating dancing dust motes in the stagnant air. You sit on the edge of a hard iron bed, staring at an empty wooden bowl on the floor. For months, you have been entirely alone in this circular room, listening only to the distant calls of birds riding the thermals outside. Suddenly, a sharp metallic click echoes through the quiet cell. The heavy iron lock on the thick oak door has disengaged, and the door swings inward just a fraction of an inch, creaking on its rusty hinges. There are no footsteps in the corridor beyond, and the evening guard shift is not due for hours. The sudden possibility of freedom makes your pulse race. How do you approach the mysteriously unlocked door?

### The Archmage's Apprentice
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult apprentice | top floor of a wizard's tower | alone | glowing crystal and open grimoire | midnight | the crystal is overheating
**FLAIR**: Crackling energy, smell of ozone, erratic shadows

Midnight has come and gone on the top floor of the wizard's tower, where you stand alone before a heavy oak pedestal. An open grimoire rests on the wood, its pages illuminated by the harsh, pulsing light of a fist-sized glowing crystal. The air is thick with the sharp smell of ozone, and erratic shadows dance wildly across the curved stone walls. Your master left you with strict instructions to monitor the focus, but the crystal is now vibrating violently, radiating a heat that blisters your skin even from a distance. A web of hairline fractures is spreading across its glowing surface, threatening a catastrophic magical detonation. You must act immediately to stabilize the energy flow, but touching it directly would be disastrous. How do you safely channel the excess power out of the fracturing crystal?

### The Signal Watcher
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult watcher | mountain signal beacon platform | alone | unlit pyre and flint | dense fog at dawn | you hear movement on the stairs below
**FLAIR**: Biting wind, eerie silence, creeping dread

Dense, damp fog rolls over the mountain signal platform, chilling you to the bone in the pale dawn light. You stand alone beside a massive, unlit pyre of pitch-soaked wood, clutching flint and a steel striker in your numb hands. The biting wind usually howls around this peak, but today the fog has brought an eerie, oppressive silence. Suddenly, that silence is broken by the distinct scrape of heavy boots on the stone spiral stairs leading up to your isolated post. No relief patrol is scheduled for another week, and the heavy footfalls sound deliberate and stealthy. The encroaching mist hides the entrance to the stairwell, leaving you completely exposed on the open platform. The intruder is only moments away from cresting the final step. Where do you position yourself to confront the unseen arrival?

### The Bellringer
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult bellringer | cathedral belfry | alone | thick hemp rope and massive iron bell | early morning | a bat is tangled in the rope
**FLAIR**: Musty air, fluttering wings, echoing silence

The musty air of the cathedral belfry fills your lungs as you prepare for the early morning call to prayer. You stand alone on the wooden ringing platform, your hands resting on the thick, worn hemp rope that vanishes into the shadows above. High overhead, the massive iron bell waits in the gloom. As you pull the rope taut to begin your first swing, a frantic squeaking and the sound of wildly fluttering wings erupt from just above your head. A large bat has become badly tangled in the coarse fibers of the rope, its sharp claws gripping the hemp in panic. If you pull the rope to ring the bell, the creature will be crushed in the mechanism, but the morning chimes must sound on time. How do you deal with the terrified animal blocking your duty?

### The Mooring Mast Attendant
**SNAPSHOT FACTS**: Adult attendant | top of an airship mooring mast | alone | leather harness and signal flags | windy afternoon | an unidentified airship is approaching fast
**FLAIR**: Flapping canvas, swaying structure, tense anticipation

You hang suspended in a heavy leather harness at the very top of a towering steel mooring mast, buffeted by the strong winds of a turbulent afternoon.
