## Non-Negotiable Constraints

The pack applies only to the live SynapticGM product. It does not introduce a second critic model, continuity model, multi-agent turn path, fine-tuning dataset, graph database, or engine redesign. Player-facing and GM-ready material uses generic folklore and original SynapticGM wording only. No third-party image archive will be included. Adult-sensitive material is flagged and softened; no sexual content involving minors is permitted.
