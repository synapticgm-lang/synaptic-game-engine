# Document 1 — Patent Attorney Instruction & Invention Disclosure Brief

**Status:** Draft — for review before reliance or filing. This instruction document is a factual briefing aid, not a patentability opinion, freedom-to-operate opinion or filing instruction.

## 1. Requested engagement

The project seeks a UK Chartered Patent Attorney with demonstrable electro-mechanical, thermal/HVAC, embedded-control and PCT prosecution experience to: (a) advise on a UK priority filing; (b) conduct or commission a focused patentability and freedom-to-operate search; (c) draft/prosecute an application and advise on registered design/trade-secret boundaries; and (d) advise whether and when a PCT application and selected national/regional phases are commercially justified. Verify the adviser on the IPReg register and use the CIPA directory for initial selection. [1] [2]

The project is an outdoor micro-climate cassette adaptable to a parasol/canopy or roof-cowl context. It combines a physically isolated annular air plenum, hydronic heat exchange, condensate management, mast routing segregation, renewable-source power routing and sensor-controlled safety/thermal logic. The design includes a 24/48 V nominal DC architecture as a technical design objective; no claim of SELV compliance, safety certification or grid-interconnection approval is made.

## 2. Invention disclosure

### Problem addressed

Existing outdoor shade or comfort products can provide shade, air movement or local heating but can lack a protected, repeatable air-distribution cassette; can produce unmanaged condensation; can expose rotating parts or strobe effects; and can route fluids/power/sensors through a mast without robust segregation. Renewable add-ons can produce waste heat or excess electrical energy without an integrated thermal path.

### Proposed technical solution

An annular cassette contains a plenum of 75 mm nominal height and a low-profile coil. The plenum is separated from a kinetic cassette and terminates in a 10 mm-equivalent perimeter slot oriented 35° inward/downward. A 3° tray collects condensate to a controlled drain. A 50 mm OD / 44 mm ID mast receives separate EPDM supply/return fluid hoses, high-draw DC conductors and shielded sensor cabling by way of a routing bushing. A controller selects hydronic/fan/wind/dump states using ambient temperature/RH, calculated dew point, coil and water temperatures, flow, leak/pan, wind and rotor data. A reservoir/PCM path can accept waste heat from a motor/conversion component and diversion energy from a wind/PV bus.
