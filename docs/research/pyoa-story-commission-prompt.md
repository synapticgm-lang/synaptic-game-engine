# Commission prompt: Pick Your Own Adventure bible

Paste this into another AI when you want a **new** SynapticGM PYOA campaign. We ingest the output as a campaign bible. Original worlds only — no licensed plots, places, or character names.

---

You are writing a **pick-your-own-adventure campaign bible** for SynapticGM, not a novel and not an open-world game.

## What this engine actually does
- Code owns facts (inventory, who is with you, what you already chose, hidden killer/clues if mystery).
- The writer only writes **this turn’s camera**: 2–6 sentences, then 3–4 numbered choices.
- The player’s typed **comments and reactions** are the main character’s inner voice.
- There is a **clear main spine**. Side work is optional and hidden until the player looks, talks, or wanders.

## Do not copy
No Fable / Albion / Heroes Guild, no named novels, anime, or games. Genre tropes only. Invent original place, NPCs, and object.

## Do not clone the last bible
Every campaign must play as a **different game**, not the same machine in a new coat.

**Banned default opening four** (unless the player types that intent):
1. Take the companion’s hand
2. Shove them as bait
3. Hide the MacGuffin
4. Tap / inject / smash the MacGuffin

**Banned default spine:** companion offer → faction A tour → faction B tour → dungeon → MacGuffin is a twist → three-way clash → deliver / keep / sell / burn / forge.

**Banned default endings:** honest+stayed HEA / sell+rival-cut-to-black / burn+stayed / forge-god-solo / hoard-alone / honest-solo-you-die — unless this genre truly needs that math.

**Unique NPC names.** Do not reuse Elara, Kaelen, Valerius, Silas, Vane, Rigg from other SynapticGM bibles.

## Genre decides the verbs
Write forks that only this story can offer:
- Mystery: search, ask who last saw them, accuse a name, lie to the law
- Survival / space: seal a door, share air, leave someone
- Romance: lie for them, leak a name, kiss or refuse
- Horror: dose or don’t, follow the sound, seal a hatch
- Stealth: blend, climb, don a disguise
- Isekai: ask what the error means, poke one hologram, talk to the guards
- Road: walk together, take the coin, help or rob at the ford

## Write all of the following

### 1. Pitch
- Title
- Tagline (one sentence)
- Starting location (a real place in the world, never the title)
- Simple genre tag (2–4 words): murder mystery, romance, alone in space, etc.

### 2. Main spine (7 beats, skeleton only)
Numbered 1–7. Unique to this genre. Beat 1 is the opening situation and a companion *offer*, not a forced take-hand.
Beats 2–4: one pressure each, not a faction checklist.
Beat 7 is a resolution keyed to **this** story’s ending logic (accusation, who is on the pod, who you still love, whether the System stays) — not a MacGuffin shop unless the genre is a heist.

### 3. Relationship stamps
- One named companion: Walks With You / Left / Rival
- Two factions or people who want something. Stamps stick. No alignment meter.

### 4. Inner voice
Player comments, jokes, and doubts are the hero thinking or speaking.

### 5. Side quest seeds (5–8)
Hidden until earned. One line each.

### 6. Several endings (at least 6)
Keyed to **this genre’s** stamps. Do not name endings in play.

### 7. Opening kit
Named clothes / one quest object. No generic iron shortsword.

### 8. Key NPCs (3–6)
Name, role, disposition, want, betrayal reaction. Unique names.

### 9. License note
Original setting, tropes only, not based on any named series.

### 10. Fork style (one paragraph)
The verbs this story’s choices must use. Include: do not offer the banned default four unless they typed that.

## Output format

```
TITLE:
TAGLINE:
GENRE TAG:
STARTING LOCATION:
OPENING HOOK: (one paragraph; last line is the situation, not “whether you take their hand”)

PREMISE: (binding rails first: inner voice, ally/betray, genre spine, sides, ending logic)

FORK STYLE:
SPINE:
ENDINGS: (6+, keyed to this genre)

LORE:
NPCS:
STARTER QUEST:
STARTER ITEM:
```

If this is a **murder mystery**, also list 6 possible culprits and 3 clue pools (weapon / scene tell / cover story). Code will pick one of each at New Game.

Keep it compact. Unique names. No licensed IP.
