## Genre examples reviewed (plots not used)

English LitRPG / isekai that use *summoning* as a machine (titles for research only):

1. Classic “kingdom summons a hero vs a demon lord” (many light novels; we take the **machine**, not a setting).
2. Unwanted / extra hero (wrong class, political leftover).
3. Hero as disposable weapon; summoners lie about the war.
4. Repeated summon as a *minion* / tool of other casters.
5. Villainess / calamity stamp: the ritual labels you the problem.
6. Portal-to-RPG-world (permanent displacement, no logout).
7. Appraisal-gated cheat skills.
8. Kingdom vs rival “demon” polity that is actually a state.
9. Double summon / spare soul hidden by the court.
10. Agency-first 2020s LitRPG: character choice over instant OP lecture.

**Not copied:** characters, place names, skill names, war maps, or scene beats from any of the above.
