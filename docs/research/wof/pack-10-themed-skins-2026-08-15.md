# WOF Pack 10 — Themed skins (same MMO bones)

**Project:** WOF only. Do not implement into live SynapticGM.  
**Status:** Catalog. Not a build ticket.  
**Point:** One engine (Place graph, party instances, EncounterLedger, ready-check rounds, Mode A/C narration, Tier 3 hubs). Many **skins** = original setting + a rules module. Genre *patterns* are not owned. Names, maps, creatures, and plots of other games/books are.

---

## Copyright fence (all skins)

**Allowed:** gameplay patterns (catch-and-bond, fellowship quest, sect ranking, instanced hunt); public-domain folklore (human, elf, dwarf, giant, dragon-as-archetype, Arthurian/Norse/Greek *stories in the public domain*); original names, maps, creatures, factions.

**Forbidden as WOF content:** another work’s unique names, places, creatures, items, slogans, maps, or beat-for-beat plots. “Like X” in the table means **genre pattern**, not a clone.

Tolkien-shaped skin ≠ Middle-earth (no Hobbit, Ring-as-that-plot, Mordor, named Istari).  
Creature-bond skin ≠ that media franchise’s monsters, balls, regions, or slogans.  
Same for every row.

This is not legal advice. When a skin is built, original bible + name ban-list in the GM prompt.

---

## Shared vs per-skin

| Shared (do not fork) | Per skin (data + rules module) |
|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Hubs + instanced combat | Places, races, creatures, quest DAGs |
| Party 2–5 / raid 10 | What the ledger resolves (HP, capture, realms, heat) |
| Simultaneous rounds | Win conditions, loot tables, lockout flavour |
| Mode A dungeon / A or C raid | Voice, System chrome name, UI labels |
| Durable hub NPCs | Faction tracks, collection log, ship/hold sheets |

A skin is a **content pack + rules flags**, not a second MMO codebase.

## Skin list

Working titles are placeholders. Swap when John names them.

### 1. Ash Compact (default WOF)
**Based around:** Classic faction-fantasy MMO *structure* (two factions, starter zones, hub towns, instanced dungeons/raids).  
**Play:** Level, quest DAG, 5-man dungeons, 10-man phase-graph raids.  
**Fence:** No licensed races, cities, or raid fights. Folklore analogs OK with original cultures.

### 2. Bonded Menagerie
**Based around:** Creature-collecting *pattern* (wild bonds, small active party, type matchups, ranch/workshop). Neighbours in spirit: pocket-monster leagues; craft-and-pal survival.  
**Play:** Capture/bond instead of (or besides) kill; ledger tracks creature HP + loyalty; raids = nest sieges / titanic fauna.  
**Fence:** Original fauna, capture tools, regions, factions. No franchise monsters, balls, dex names, gym slogans, or lookalike mascots.

### 3. First-Song
**Based around:** Mythic fellowship *pattern* (mixed peoples, a long war, a corrupting relic *archetype*). Neighbour in spirit: high-legend quest epics.  
**Play:** Reputation with courts; raids = named evils with phase scripts; travel edges matter.  
**Fence:** Public-domain races/archetypes only. No Middle-earth toponyms, unique species, or that ring-plot beat-for-beat.

### 4. Sect Ascension
**Based around:** Cultivation / wuxia *pattern* (sects, closed-door training, tournament brackets, realm breakthroughs).  
**Play:** Ledger can gate actions by realm; dungeons = secret realms; raids = sect war set-pieces.  
**Fence:** Original sects, techniques, realm names. No licensed novel/game sect lists.

### 5. Quarry Pact
**Based around:** Coordinated monster-hunt *pattern* (4–5 hunters, weak points, carve loot).  
**Play:** Party dungeon *is* the hunt; raid 10 = elder quarry with soak/interrupt flags.  
**Fence:** Original fauna and hunt grounds. No licensed monster names, weapons, or village brands.

### 6. Gridrun
**Based around:** Cyberpunk crew *pattern* (street hub, contracted runs, corp heat).  
**Play:** Dungeons = facilities; raids = blacksites; heat track on the party.  
**Fence:** Original city, corps, chrome. No licensed megacorp names or that one night city’s districts.

### 7. Blackwake
**Based around:** Age-of-sail / pirate *pattern* (ports, crews, boarding).  
**Play:** Ship is a Place; boarding = instance; raid = fortress or leviathan.  
**Fence:** Original seas and flags. No licensed pirate-franchise characters or cursed-flag plots.

### 8. Lanceyard
**Based around:** Mecha lance *pattern* (4–5 machines, heat/ammo, battlefield instance).  
**Play:** Pilot + frame sheets; raid 10 = two lances vs a fortress engine.  
**Fence:** Original frames and theatres. No licensed mech series names or distinctive silhouettes described as those mechs.

### 9. Night Charter
**Based around:** Hidden-society *pattern* (one city, politics, hunger, masquerade).  
**Play:** Hubs are courts; dungeons = hunts/havens; raids = city-shaking rites. Social heat > trash mobs.  
**Fence:** Original courts and bloodlines. No licensed clan names, that one World-of-darkness toolkit, or trademarked splat lists.

### 10. Badge Circuit
**Based around:** Superhero-patrol *pattern* (city zones, civilian presence, instanced hideouts).  
**Play:** Street = shared hub with traces; instances = villain lairs; raid = city crisis script.  
**Fence:** Original city and capes. No licensed heroes, publishers’ cities, or slogan clones.

### 11. Dust Line
**Based around:** Frontier western *pattern* (towns, posses, hideouts).  
**Play:** Posse = party; hideout = dungeon; raid = fort or rail siege.  
**Fence:** Original territories. No licensed cowboy IPs or that one gang’s plot.

### 12. Veil Watch
**Based around:** Cosmic-horror investigation *pattern* (clues, sanity/steadfast track, set-piece horrors).  
**Play:** Dungeons = sites; raids = convergences. Combat is rarer; ledger may track steadfast.  
**Fence:** Original mythos. Prefer not to use famous public-domain monster *branding as the product identity*; write new horrors. No Chaosium/CoC product trademarks.

### 13. Void Reach
**Based around:** Space-opera *pattern* (stations as hubs, boarding as dungeons, fleet set-piece as raid).  
**Play:** Ship/station Places; faction reputation; instanced derelicts.  
**Fence:** Original polities and Force-like systems under new metaphysics. No licensed orders, empires, or sword-religion names.

### 14. Hollow Term
**Based around:** Magic-school *pattern* (houses, terms, trials).  
**Play:** Campus hub; trial instances; raid = end-of-term calamity.  
**Fence:** Original school, houses, spells. No licensed boy-wizard world, houses, or artifacts.

### 15. Crew Score
**Based around:** Heist-crew *pattern* (planning in a hub, timed instance, roles as code flags).  
**Play:** Dungeon = the job; raid 10 = vault or convoy. Mode C fits (numbers + phase callouts).  
**Fence:** Original city and targets. No licensed crime-franchise crews or bank-job brands.

### 16. Hearth Ruin
**Based around:** Post-collapse salvage *pattern* (scarcity, holdings, ruin delves).  
**Play:** Closest to live SynapticGM DNA; WOF instances on top.  
**Fence:** Original collapse. No licensed vault-corp, that one wasteland IP, or named mascots. **Do not ship this skin into the live game** — live already occupies this lane.

## What a skin must ship (when built)

1. Original name bible + GM **ban-list** of lookalike proper nouns  
2. Place graph + 4 starter zones (or equivalent hubs)  
3. Rules flags: what EncounterLedger resolves  
4. 18–25 quest beats / starter zone (Pack 8c)  
5. One 5-man instance + one 10-man 3-phase script  

No extra multiplayer research required to pick a skin. Bonded Menagerie is the only skin that needs a **capture/bond ledger** design pass before build (not another MMO-presence dump).
