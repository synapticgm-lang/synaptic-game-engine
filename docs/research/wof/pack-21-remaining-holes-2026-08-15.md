## Holes that still change the idea

### 1) What a “turn” costs in a group (biggest)

Free = 15 turns/day. A 5-man is ~15–25 rounds. A raid night is ~40–60 rounds. If one lockstep round burns **one turn per player**, free players cannot finish a dungeon, and mid-sub (~50) barely raids.

Need an explicit rule, for example:

- Hub talk / story beat = 1 turn (that player only)  
- Lockstep round = 1 turn each (or 1 shared, split)  
- Raid Mode C (short chrome) costs less than Mode A  

Until this is named, pricing and “raid in v1” fight each other.

### 2) WOF vs live SynapticGM

Same app or a second product? Same account? Does buying WOF chrome show up in the solo LitRPG? Isekai Gate must **not** ship into live. Players will assume one game. This is a store/brand hole, not a combat hole.

### 3) Ten people on a phone

5-man HUD is sketched. Raid 10 needs: 10 names, soak/interrupt, plan-auto Stop, late prose, disconnect Hold — **thumb reach**. If this fails, raid-in-v1 is the wrong lock.

### 4) Death, repair, and gold sinks

Bleed-out exists in a maths one-pager. MMO needs: wipe vs character death, repair, durability, corpse run (checkpoint already locked). Without this, gold sinks stay fictional.

### 5) Family / Kid Mode LLM bill

Kid Mode blocks IAP. Kids still burn turns. Who pays — parent sub, shared family pool, or Kid Mode is text-only / lower cap?

### 6) After week 2 with zero friends

Friends-first finder is locked. Solo-able first dungeon is a dump pick. Public LFG is “later” with no shape. Retention hole for strangers.

### 7) Live ops you can staff

Catalog hotfix, disable a quest, ban-list after a model swap, chat report SLA, gold rollback. Schemas exist; the **human loop** does not.

### 8) Push / mail / “raid is starting”

`MailDigest` exists. Mobile MMO without: AH sold, farm tick, party invite, lockout reset — people log off and never come back.
