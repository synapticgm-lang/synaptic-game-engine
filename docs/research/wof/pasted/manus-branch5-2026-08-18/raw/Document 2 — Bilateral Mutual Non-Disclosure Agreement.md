# Document 2 — Bilateral Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Draft — review before signature.** This Agreement is intended for execution by the legal entities signing the execution page. It is governed by the laws of England and Wales. It is a working commercial draft, not formal legal advice.

## Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on the date of the last signature below between each legal entity that signs this Agreement, each a **Party** and together the **Parties**.

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate, discuss, design, quote for, prototype, test, manufacture, license, integrate, finance or otherwise collaborate in relation to an outdoor micro-climate hardware project and related clean-technology products, including a hybrid SELV micro-climate cassette, its mechanical structures, thermal loops, power systems, control electronics, CAD, firmware, prototypes, field trials and commercialisation (the **Purpose**). No Party is required to disclose information or proceed with any transaction.

### 2. Confidential Information

**Confidential Information** means all information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party, whether before, on or after the date of this Agreement, in written, electronic, oral, visual, physical or machine-readable form, that is marked confidential, stated to be confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes inventions, patent applications, know-how, trade secrets, drawings, CAD files, OpenSCAD files, source code, firmware, algorithms, configurations, bills of materials, designs, prototypes, test fixtures, test data, product roadmaps, specifications, safety analyses, supplier and customer details, commercial terms, pricing, manufacturing methods, tooling, quality records, deployment sites, photographs, samples, credentials and the existence and terms of discussions.

Confidential Information also includes analyses, notes, copies, extracts, prototypes and materials created by a Receiving Party that contain, reflect or are derived from the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by contemporaneous written evidence: (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information; (d) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (e) is required to be disclosed by law, court order, regulator or stock-exchange rule, provided that the Receiving Party gives prior written notice where legally permitted and discloses only what is required.

### 4. Confidentiality obligations

A Party receiving Confidential Information (**Receiving Party**) shall: (a) use it solely for the Purpose; (b) protect it with safeguards no less protective than those used for its own confidential information of similar importance and in no event less than reasonable care; (c) disclose it only to its employees, directors, professional advisers, financiers, insurers and subcontractors who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written or professional confidentiality duties no less protective than this Agreement; (d) maintain appropriate access control, version control, secure storage and transmission; (e) not reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, photograph, scan, copy or test any prototype or software except to the extent expressly authorised in writing for the Purpose; and (f) promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any actual or suspected unauthorised access, use or disclosure.

### 5. Prototype, CAD and firmware controls

A Receiving Party shall keep any prototype, sample, controlled drawing, CAD model, source repository access, credential, firmware image or test data in a secure location and shall maintain a custody record. A Receiving Party shall not connect, energise, test, modify, reproduce, photograph or transfer a prototype except under the Disclosing Party’s written test, safety and custody instructions. No Party may upload controlled CAD, firmware, drawings or specifications to public, consumer or unapproved cloud services, model-training systems or code repositories.

### 6. Non-circumvention

For three years from the date of the last signature, neither Party shall knowingly bypass the other Party to enter a transaction directly with a supplier, installer, pilot customer, OEM, manufacturer, distributor, funder or commercial contact first introduced in writing by the other Party specifically for the Purpose (a **Protected Contact**), where the transaction concerns the Purpose or a substantially similar project. This clause does not prohibit dealings with a contact that the Receiving Party can show it knew and was actively pursuing before the written introduction, public tender participation, or independently sourced dealings unrelated to the Purpose. A Party proposing a direct transaction with a Protected Contact shall first give the introducing Party written notice and a reasonable opportunity to participate. This clause is intended as a proportionate project-protection provision and shall be interpreted only to the extent lawful and enforceable under applicable competition law.

### 7. Ownership, no licence and improvements

Each Party retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property, Confidential Information, materials and know-how. No licence, assignment, option, implied right, joint ownership or authority to use a Party’s intellectual property is granted except the limited, revocable right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. No Party may file, procure or support a patent, design, trade mark, domain name or other right incorporating the other Party’s Confidential Information without prior written consent.

Any jointly developed invention, design, software, data set or prototype deliverable shall be dealt with only in a separate written agreement signed by both Parties. Unless and until such agreement is signed, no joint-development activity is authorised to transfer ownership or grant a licence.

### 8. Return, deletion and retention

On written request or when discussions end, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or securely destroy the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information, including samples, drawings, source files, credentials, copies and derived materials, and shall confirm compliance in writing. The Receiving Party may retain one archival copy only where required for legal, regulatory, insurance or audit purposes, subject to continuing confidentiality and access limitation. Automatically generated disaster-recovery backups may be retained until overwritten in the ordinary course, provided they are not restored except for disaster recovery and remain protected.

### 9. Required disclosure and protected disclosures

Nothing in this Agreement requires a Party to withhold information where disclosure is legally required, prevents lawful protected disclosures, whistleblowing, reporting of crime, regulatory cooperation or the exercise of rights that cannot lawfully be restricted. A Party shall, where lawful, give prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation to seek confidential treatment or limit scope.

### 10. No warranty and no obligation

Confidential Information is supplied “as is”. The Disclosing Party gives no representation that it is accurate, complete, non-infringing, safe, fit for a particular purpose, commercially viable or free from third-party rights. Neither Party is obliged to enter a definitive agreement, make an investment, place an order, grant a licence or continue discussions.

### 11. Term and survival

This Agreement starts on the date of the last signature and continues for three years. The obligations in Clauses 4 to 10 survive for three years after termination or expiry. In respect of trade secrets and source code that remain genuinely secret and are protected through reasonable measures, the use and disclosure restrictions survive for so long as the information remains a trade secret or confidential under applicable law.

### 12. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorised use or disclosure may cause harm for which damages alone may be inadequate. The Disclosing Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief available from a court, in addition to any other remedy. Nothing in this clause requires a court to grant relief or limits a court’s discretion.

### 13. Data protection, export and compliance

Each Party shall comply with laws applicable to its handling of personal data, export-controlled information, sanctions, workplace safety and anti-bribery. Neither Party shall transfer personal data unless the transfer is necessary for the Purpose and an appropriate legal basis and safeguards are in place. No Party may use Confidential Information in a manner that breaches applicable law or product-safety obligations.

### 14. Assignment, notices and entire agreement

Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other Party’s written consent, except to a successor in a merger, reorganisation or sale of substantially all relevant assets that agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement. Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, recognised courier or email to the contact details stated by each signing Party in the execution page; an email notice is effective when no delivery-failure message is received.

This Agreement is the entire agreement concerning confidentiality for the Purpose and supersedes earlier confidentiality discussions about the Purpose. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect and the invalid provision shall be modified only to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

### 15. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising from it are governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction, except that either Party may seek urgent interim or protective relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.
