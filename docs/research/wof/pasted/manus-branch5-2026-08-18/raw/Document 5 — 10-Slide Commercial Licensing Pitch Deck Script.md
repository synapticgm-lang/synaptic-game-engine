# Document 5 — 10-Slide Commercial Licensing Pitch Deck Script

**Use condition:** This is a licensing conversation script for commercial canopy OEMs and outdoor-hospitality brands. Do not state “validated,” “patent pending,” “certified,” “registered design,” energy yield, CFM, Delta-T, market size, margin or regulatory approval unless the cited test, filing, registration or commercial evidence exists at presentation time.

| Slide | Title / visual layout | Speaker script | Evidence gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The All-Season Outdoor Micro-Climate**. Full-bleed original concept render with exploded cassette inset. | “We are developing a modular micro-climate cassette intended to make premium outdoor space more usable across heat, still air and cooler conditions. The proposition is not a generator bolted to a parasol. It is a protected airflow, thermal-routing and control platform designed to fit a canopy or architectural-cowl envelope.” | Use conceptual rendering marked “development concept” until field proof. |
| 2 | **The problem is not shade alone.** Four icon blocks: strobe/flicker, unmanaged drip, exposed moving systems, seasonal downtime. | “Operators face discomfort, weather variability and equipment that is either visually intrusive, hard to service or usable for one season. Uncontrolled coil condensation and poorly integrated moving hardware create operational risk. The commercial question is whether a repeatable cassette can add comfort without adding an unmanageable maintenance or liability burden.” | Use customer interviews/site observations; do not claim prevalence without source. |
| 3 | **Universal Cassette solution.** Layered cross-section: guard/kinetic layer, 75 mm plenum, coil/tray, radial diffuser, mast/base. | “The cassette separates the air path from kinetic hardware. A low-profile hydronic coil sits in a nominal 75 mm plenum. A perimeter diffuser directs flow inward and downward. A sloped tray manages condensate deliberately. The same platform can support controlled fan, hydronic heating/cooling and carefully bounded renewable/thermal-routing options.” | Display engineering screen diagram; mark all dimensions as design basis. |
| 4 | **Underlying technology and architecture.** Simple P&ID + DC-bus diagram; one callout each for dew point, waste heat and safety layers. | “The system treats moisture, flow, wind and energy as linked control inputs. The controller is intended to restrict cooling near dew point, prove flow before conditioned operation, route available waste heat into the thermal loop and enter a safe stow/fault state on gust, leak, pan or sensor events. Critical protection is layered; firmware is not the sole safety barrier.” | Show versioned architecture and stated test plan, not compliance claims. |
| 5 | **Evidence roadmap: simulation → benchtop → full-scale pilot.** Three-stage horizontal chart. | “The present evidence base is a parametric screening model and a controlled Phase 0 test protocol. We will measure slot velocity, pressure drop, water-side thermal duty, air Delta-T, condensation onset and fault response. Only after the controlled rig meets its acceptance criteria will we publish performance values or make customer-facing delivery claims.” | Insert measured values only with test ID/date/configuration and uncertainty. |
| 6 | **Defensible know-how and IP route.** Claim tree graphic: apparatus 1–10; system/control 11–15; side bar for design/trade secret. | “The working claim structure covers the multi-layer cassette, plenum/diffuser/tray, mast segregation and energy/thermal control path. A UK filing decision is pending qualified attorney review. Beyond patent claims, the commercial moat includes CAD interfaces, visual design, test fixtures, calibration and integration know-how. We will not describe any right as granted or pending until it is formally so.” | Display filing status exactly as recorded in IP register. |
| 7 | **First markets and licensing fit.** Four segments: hospitality, contract canopy, glamping/luxury patio, architectural cowl. | “We are not launching first into mass residential retail. The initial fit is an OEM or operator with installed service, fabrication and warranty capability. The first objective is a paid or strategically sponsored pilot in a controlled hospitality or contract setting, followed by a defined field licence rather than a broad global grant.” | Support each segment with named, permissioned discovery evidence. |
| 8 | **Unit economics must pass the licence test.** Waterfall: OEM net sales → COGS/logistics/warranty/service → contribution → IP economics. | “The product has to leave room for installation, field service, warranty, certification and channel margin before royalty. We will supply a transparent configuration-level BOM and stress costs at prototype, pilot and production volumes. At this stage, figures are planning allowances subject to supplier RFQs and test-driven design changes.” | Never present unquoted BOM allowances as supplier quotations. |
| 9 | **Commercial licensing model.** Term-sheet visual: field, territory, NRE, milestones, royalty, minimum, audit, safety/change control. | “The proposed structure is a staged development agreement followed by a field-limited licence. The commercial package may combine NRE, prototype/pilot milestones, an auditable royalty on defined net sales and minimum performance obligations. A 3%–7% royalty range is an initial negotiating envelope—not a market fact or final term—and must reflect exclusivity, patent scope, know-how, volume and OEM contribution.” | Use attorney-reviewed term sheet; show all deductions and audit terms. |
| 10 | **Partnership call to action.** 12–18-month visual: diligence, bench proof, prototype, pilot, validation, licence decision. | “We seek one or two partners willing to contribute manufacturing expertise, pilot access and market feedback under controlled confidentiality. The immediate ask is a technical diligence session and a scoped development/pilot discussion. No party is asked to commit to a volume licence before the hardware, safety and field evidence meet agreed gates.” | Attach request list, NDA and phased development plan. |

## Layout system

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Visual language | Original technical diagrams, own CAD renders and generic material/airflow illustrations; no licensed canopy, manga, brand or competitor imagery. |
| Colour | Deep blue/charcoal for structural/energy layer, teal for hydronic path, amber for thermal diversion, red only for fault/safety. |
| Text | One proposition per slide; no dense legal text; use an evidence footnote where claims are preliminary. |
| Numbers | Every value needs a source/test ID/date or the label “screening assumption”/“planning allowance.” |
| Technical claims | Avoid “self-powering,” “all-season,” “zero-drip,” “safe,” “SELV-compliant,” “grid-tie,” “patent-pending” and “validated” unless defined evidence exists. |

## Commercial term-sheet starting positions

| Term | Proposed opening position | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Licence field | Commercial canopies/outdoor hospitality for first partner. | Prevents surrender of architectural-cowl/other fields. |
| Territory | One launch territory or defined OEM channel. | Keeps options until demand/qualification is proved. |
| Exclusivity | Conditional on paid development, launch date, annual minimum and safety/quality compliance. | Converts to non-exclusive on missed milestones. |
| NRE | Partner funds defined engineering, fixtures and test work. | Avoids unfunded bespoke development. |
| Royalty | 3%–7% of tightly defined licensed-product net sales, subject to negotiation. | Starting envelope only; audit and deductions are as important as rate. |
| Minimum guarantee | Pilot-stage launch fee/capacity reservation, then ramping annual minimum after production proof. | Avoids arbitrary early burden but preserves opportunity cost. |
| Data | Licensor receives anonymised reliability/performance/field-failure data. | Supports continued design and safety improvement. |
| Change control | No design/process/component change without agreed interface and verification procedure. | Maintains test evidence and IP coherence. |

Licensing should define field, territory, exclusivity, improvements, payment mechanics and auditability rather than rely on a headline royalty alone. [1] [2]
