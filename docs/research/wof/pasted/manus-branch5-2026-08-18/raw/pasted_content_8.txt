### SYSTEM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY

The invention consists of a multi-layer climate control system operating in two distinct deployment models:
1. Portable All-Weather Patio Parasol / Micro-Climate Pod
2. Residential Architectural Smart Roof Cowl / HVAC Turret

#### Core Sub-systems & Mechanical Architecture:
1. Active Kinetic / Aerodynamic Layer:
   - Low-profile, pitched aerodynamic fan blades positioned at the top of the assembly.
   - Dual-Mode Direct-Drive Brushless System: Operates as a motorized downward fan (cooling) or as a vertical-axis wind turbine / kinetic harvester (power generation).
   - Dynamic Delta-Wye (Δ-Y) Electronic Switching: Automatically switches generator coil wiring between Star (Wye) for high-voltage/low-wind trickle charging and Delta for high-amperage/storm-level kinetic harvesting.
   - Dynamic electronic magnetic braking/governor for storm-level overspeed protection.
   - Slip Ring Assembly: Multi-channel rotary electrical joint transferring energy from rotating blade-mounted solar cells and sensors to the stationary central chassis.

2. Aerodynamic Plenum & Radial Air Distribution:
   - Stationary Blackout Shield: Mounted 2 to 4 inches directly below the rotating blades, acting as a physical safety barrier, eliminating sunlight strobing/flicker, and providing permanent shade.
   - Pressurized Plenum Chamber: The 2-4 inch gap between blades and shield acts as an air compression chamber.
   - Perimeter Diffuser Ring ("Guttering"): Air is forced radially outward to a perimeter rim equipped with multi-angled perforation holes (30°–45° inward pitch) to envelope users in an evenly distributed thermal zone rather than a direct downward blast.

3. Closed-Loop Hydronic (AIO) Thermal Sub-system:
   - Liquid-to-Air Heat Exchanger (Radiator): Located inside the pressurized plenum chamber.
   - Summer Cooling Mode: Low-power 12V pump circulates ice-chilled water/fluid from an insulated reservoir in the weighted base to the plenum radiator, cooling the intake air before perimeter diffusion. Built-in condensation slope and drain channel.
   - Winter Heating & Waste-Heat Recovery Mode: Liquid loop circulates through a heat-scavenging jacket on the generator housing (absorbing coil heat generated by Back-EMF resistance) and along the backside of radiant Infrared (IR) heating panels mounted beneath the stationary shield. Includes an automated dump-load immersion heating element inside the base reservoir to convert storm-level wind power directly into thermal mass.
   - Fluid Chemistry: Propylene glycol-water mixture for sub-zero anti-freeze protection.

4. Enclosure & Power Infrastructure:
   - Modular Weather Enclosure: Detachable, weighted, marine-grade clear vinyl curtains with bottom-edge exhaust ventilation to prevent aerodynamic ballooning and ensure continuous fresh-air displacement.
   - Base Architecture: Serves as low-center-of-gravity ballast housing the motor/generator drive, battery storage, fluid reservoir, pump, and smart hybrid inverter.
   - Power & Grid-Tie Architecture: Smart automatic transfer switch prioritizing on-board solar/wind battery power for baseline loads (pump, fan, controls) and dynamically supplementing mains power when high-draw IR elements engage.
   - Residential Adaptability: Scales to a roof-mounted turret interfacing with attic plenum boxes, room diffusers, and geothermal ground-loop heat exchangers.
