## Shared invariants

All 23 rows are skins over one typed engine. The engine owns dice, HP, status effects, quest ticks, loot, gold, lockouts, instance seeds, wallet balances, travel costs, and entitlement checks; prose is generated only after committed state. Overworld presence exposes `nearbyPlayerCount` and race tags only. Combat is instanced and lockstep, with parties of 2–5, personal loot, weekly per-character per-boss lockouts, checkpoint return on wipe, and no mid-combat fill. Raid 10 is Mid+ only where the row explicitly permits it; no row changes the ordinary party cap.

Every world retains two distinct wallets: one gameplay wallet for earned currency and one cosmetic wallet for appearance purchases. No skin label authorizes selling outcomes, lockout skips, catch-rate packs, raid clears, random power, gacha, loot boxes, or stat power. Gold analogs cannot purchase cosmetic-only grants through a merged balance, and cosmetic analogs cannot pay for repairs, travel, upkeep, or gameplay rewards.

Housing remains private and friends-only. Guests cannot loot chests; owners can remove guests; furniture has no combat power. The shared upkeep rule is one weekly charge, one-week lockout after nonpayment, and NPC seizure in week three. Inn binding is one turn on the first bind and free on later same-region board use; ferry and coach exits remain data-defined place transitions, never teleports.

Kid Mode remains ten text turns per day, with parent sharing from the family pool. The extra-ban column is additive and applies to rewrite-or-skip moderation; it never weakens the shared bans on public DMs, public trade, voice, gambling, sexual content, gore, drugs, or unsafe real-world instructions. English v1, quiet-hours essential push, no global chat, and no save import from live SynapticGM apply to every row.

## Rename and IP guardrails

These rows introduce no new species, factions, regions, maps, quest IDs, licensed references, or production paths. `Tide Covenant` remains a faction; `Hearthborn`, `Lanternfolk`, `Saltkin`, and `Stonevein` remain races only in Ash Compact. The dumped First-Song string “Gloam Court Siege” is not used as a public title; its replacement is **The Cinder-Court Break**. The Bonded Menagerie starter big night is the five-person **Fair**; Migration Night 10 is optional Mid+ content. The Bonded Menagerie internal kit rename and the Isekai rules-module slug belong in the dedicated rename table and are not altered by these display-only rows.

No row claims an unshipped MMO. Player-facing labels remain **solo**, **private co-op**, and **limited online region**. No row touches live SynapticGM clocks, data, accounts, or source paths.
