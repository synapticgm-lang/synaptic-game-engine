# WOF Scale Era: Bespoke Press Bill

## Store identity

**One-line pitch.** A field-expedition valley where cataloguers, trail hands, shelter readers, and boneglass cutters learn how original giant animals reshape a changing landscape.

Scale Era begins at **Basalt Shelter**, where **Scale Era: Name a Working Promise** asks the player to make a practical choice before anything grand occurs. The **Track Scribe** kit sees that problem from a specific working angle, while the route toward **The Thunderstep Traverse** introduces a private 2–5 session that is clear about its stakes and its limits. The store page sells a buy-and-own text world for solo/private co-op, not an MMO. Its included **Scale Era Theme Kit** changes presentation without changing outcomes. Gold is **Dawn Marks**; cosmetic tokens are **Fossil Flakes**. Neither currency buys power, a better roll, a catch, a clear, or a lockout bypass.

## Legal and trust

It is not a dinosaur franchise park, a survival-crafting clone, or a fossil museum reconstruction. Canned hub lines are authored and not stranger LLM chat. Report, mute, and block are required support flows. Hashed telemetry only; TTS reads chrome and prose; font scaling and non-colour danger indicators are mandatory. Kid Mode, when eligible, limits play to ten text turns per day and disables public DM, trade, and voice.

## Press blurb

Scale Era is a WOF text world about a field-expedition valley where cataloguers, trail hands, shelter readers, and boneglass cutters learn how original giant animals reshape a changing landscape. It begins at Basalt Shelter with Scale Era: Name a Working Promise, a small commitment that makes the local texture immediately useful. Choose one of four distinct kits—including the Track Scribe—then follow clear, committed choices through a private solo or 2–5 player co-op session. The named five-room instance, The Thunderstep Traverse, uses room-first description and personal loot; the closing Long Dawn Migration gives friends a cosmetic-focused big night rather than a scaled raid claim. A complete Scale Era Theme Kit is included with the world. Gold, Dawn Marks, and cosmetic tokens, Fossil Flakes, remain separate. No gacha, outcome sale, lockout skip, or paid power item is offered. It is not described as an MMO until that capability is proven.
