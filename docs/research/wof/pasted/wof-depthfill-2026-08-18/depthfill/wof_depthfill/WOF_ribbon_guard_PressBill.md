# WOF Ribbon Guard: Bespoke Press Bill

## Store identity

**One-line pitch.** Color-team rescue show and civic cheer.

Ribbon Guard begins at **Bright Base**, where **Ribbon Guard: Name a Working Promise** asks the player to make a practical choice before anything grand occurs. The **Pose Captain** kit sees that problem from a specific working angle, while the route toward **The Stage Vault Blackout** introduces a private 2–5 session that is clear about its stakes and its limits. The store page sells a buy-and-own text world for solo/private co-op, not an MMO. Its included **Ribbon Guard Theme Kit** changes presentation without changing outcomes. Gold is **Bright Tickets**; cosmetic tokens are **Prism Pins**. Neither currency buys power, a better roll, a catch, a clear, or a lockout bypass.

## Legal and trust

It is an original WOF setting and not a licensed, historical, or platform-derived recreation. Canned hub lines are authored and not stranger LLM chat. Report, mute, and block are required support flows. Hashed telemetry only; TTS reads chrome and prose; font scaling and non-colour danger indicators are mandatory. Kid Mode, when eligible, limits play to ten text turns per day and disables public DM, trade, and voice.

## Press blurb

Ribbon Guard is a WOF text world about color-team rescue show and civic cheer. It begins at Bright Base with Ribbon Guard: Name a Working Promise, a small commitment that makes the local texture immediately useful. Choose one of four distinct kits—including the Pose Captain—then follow clear, committed choices through a private solo or 2–5 player co-op session. The named five-room instance, The Stage Vault Blackout, uses room-first description and personal loot; the closing Prism Park Rescue Revue gives friends a cosmetic-focused big night rather than a scaled raid claim. A complete Ribbon Guard Theme Kit is included with the world. Gold, Bright Tickets, and cosmetic tokens, Prism Pins, remain separate. No gacha, outcome sale, lockout skip, or paid power item is offered. It is not described as an MMO until that capability is proven.
