# WOF Threshold Rooms: Bespoke Press Bill

## Store identity

**One-line pitch.** Consentful liminal navigation and grounded scares.

Threshold Rooms begins at **Welcome Desk**, where **Threshold Rooms: Name a Working Promise** asks the player to make a practical choice before anything grand occurs. The **Anchor Monitor** kit sees that problem from a specific working angle, while the route toward **The Wrong-Floor Return** introduces a private 2–5 session that is clear about its stakes and its limits. The store page sells a buy-and-own text world for solo/private co-op, not an MMO. Its included **Threshold Rooms Theme Kit** changes presentation without changing outcomes. Gold is **Exit Stamps**; cosmetic tokens are **Comfort Threads**. Neither currency buys power, a better roll, a catch, a clear, or a lockout bypass.

## Legal and trust

It is an original WOF setting and not a licensed, historical, or platform-derived recreation. Canned hub lines are authored and not stranger LLM chat. Report, mute, and block are required support flows. Hashed telemetry only; TTS reads chrome and prose; font scaling and non-colour danger indicators are mandatory. Kid Mode, when eligible, limits play to ten text turns per day and disables public DM, trade, and voice.

## Press blurb

Threshold Rooms is a WOF text world about consentful liminal navigation and grounded scares. It begins at Welcome Desk with Threshold Rooms: Name a Working Promise, a small commitment that makes the local texture immediately useful. Choose one of four distinct kits—including the Anchor Monitor—then follow clear, committed choices through a private solo or 2–5 player co-op session. The named five-room instance, The Wrong-Floor Return, uses room-first description and personal loot; the closing Quiet Atrium Lights-Out gives friends a cosmetic-focused big night rather than a scaled raid claim. A complete Threshold Rooms Theme Kit is included with the world. Gold, Exit Stamps, and cosmetic tokens, Comfort Threads, remain separate. No gacha, outcome sale, lockout skip, or paid power item is offered. It is not described as an MMO until that capability is proven.
