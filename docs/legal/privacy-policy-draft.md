## Who we are

SynapticGM (“we”) provides an AI-assisted game. This notice explains what we collect and why.

## What we collect

Depending on how you play:

- **Account data:** email, display name, auth provider IDs (e.g. Google sign-in).
- **Gameplay / saves:** cloud save data, campaign progress when sync is enabled.
- **Billing:** handled by payment processors (e.g. Stripe); we store entitlements (tier, packs, cosmetics) and payment references needed for support — not full card numbers.
- **Ops / support:** feedback messages, moderation/flagged AI logs, telemetry (errors, turn timing) when enabled.
- **Device / technical:** approximate device info, IP (via hosting), browser type.
- **Ads:** ad networks may process device/advertising identifiers under their policies; Kid Mode should use child-appropriate / non-personalised configurations where we offer ads.

## Why we process data

- provide and secure the Service;
- deliver purchases and subscriptions;
- customer support and abuse prevention;
- improve reliability and content safety;
- legal obligations (tax, accounting).

Legal bases (UK GDPR) typically include contract, legitimate interests (security, product improvement), and consent where required (e.g. certain cookies/ads).

## Children

Kid Mode is a content filter. Parents/guardians who set a PIN are responsible for PIN security and purchases unlocked with that PIN (see Terms). We do not knowingly sell personal data. Family ad settings should be used for Kid Mode ads where available.

## Sharing

We share data with processors who help run the Service (hosting, auth, payments, AI providers, ad networks, email) under contracts. We do not sell your personal data.

## Retention

We keep account and entitlement records while your account is active and as needed for tax/legal. You may request deletion subject to legal retention needs.
