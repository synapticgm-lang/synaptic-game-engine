TITLE: The Return
TEXT: Cold brine floods the stairs, tasting of old iron and weed-flowers, rising until it laps against your chin and lifts you clear of the stone. Above, the great cog-wheels of the Meridian Gear groan once—a long, rusted sigh like a dying leviathan—before they lock forever in their bronze sockets. The seabed drinks the deluge slowly at first, then all at once as the grey water rushes back into every gutter, cellar, and cove with a clean, shivering roar. Gullmouth wakes to the sound of its own harbour wall taking the blow, boats slamming hard against their mooring ropes, and the smell of wet salt blowing up the cliffs. You drift on the surface of the green surge, staring up through the salt-crusted salt-glass of the skylights as the sun breaks through a low grey cloud. The sea has come home, and the world goes back to waiting for the tide.
