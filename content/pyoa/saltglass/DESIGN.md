# The Last Lamp Across the Saltglass - design bible

Slug `saltglass`, prefix `sg`. Pure-choice PYOA gamebook, no dice, no stats, about 1,000 sections. Written on Gemini Flash-Lite
(model verified on the mode picker before every call; never Pro or Flash). Setting, cast and plot are original; only structure
(hubs, chokepoints, flag payoffs, soft-fail endings) is taken from old gamebooks, never their text.
Not registered in `src/data/pyoa/*`: it is not in the game's live book list. Drafts live only under `content/pyoa/saltglass/`.

## Premise
The Saltglass Basin is a dried inland sea whose floor is fused glass and salt, studded with the broken glass cities of the Old Lensfolk.
Candlemere, a lamp-town on the western rim, has lost its harvests. You are a runner of the Ember-Lamp Post, entrusted with the
Seedkey, which opens the Rain Vault under Prism, the observatory-city at the Basin's heart: the last drought-proof seed hall.
The monsoon (the Return) floods the Basin in twenty days. Rivals: the Salt Court (toll-lords who buy wells and want the vault),
the Dune Sisters (well-keepers with a binding oath), the Glasswrights (mirror-signal sentries of Prism).
Tone: bright, hard, mirage-and-salt, with dry humour in the people. Second person, present tense, British spelling.

## Structure (generated by scripts/pyoa/salt-skeleton.mjs, 1000 sections)
Prologue (4) -> six regions -> finale selector -> three stances -> eight endings (3 sections each) + twelve soft-fail dooms.
1 Candlemere and the Rim Road; 2 The Bitterflats; 3 The Shard Cities; 4 The Mirror Storm; 5 Prism; 6 The Rain Vault.
Each region: in-scene, three lattice segments (6 layers: narrow -> wide -> narrow, every node reachable, <=4 exits), two mandatory
chokepoint scenes (the reader decides, or the road splits on an earlier decision), and an out-scene. Two dooms per region hang off
risky choices in the later segments.

## Flags (8) and their payoffs
| flag | set at | values | read by |
|---|---|---|---|
| guide | R1 choke 1 | pell / alone | privileged scenes R2+; R5 choke split; ending B1/B2 |
| lamp | R1 choke 2, R4 choke 2 | lit / dark | privileged scenes; endings C2/C3 |
| oath | R2 choke 1 | sworn / free | privileged scenes R3+; R6 lock split; ending A1/A2 |
| debt | R2 choke 2 | owed / clear | privileged scenes; endings C1-C3 |
| rumor | R3 choke 1 | true / false | privileged scenes R4+; R6 seed-hall split |
| tongue | R3 choke 2 | y / n | privileged scenes; R5 sentry split |
| key | R4 choke 1 | whole / cracked | privileged scenes R5+; endings A1-A3 |
| scar | R4 choke 2 | y | privileged scenes R5+ |
About 45 "privileged" lattice scenes are reachable only with the matching flag state: each is a boon, a shortcut or an insight, and its prose
names why the reader is there. Phase 2 (if quota allows): short flag-gated inserts on later pages as variants.

## Prose rules
80-150 words per section, 1-2 paragraphs, no game words (no "choose", "section", "flag", dice, stats). Exit labels are short imperatives
(3-9 words) naming the action, not the destination id. Chokepoint scenes make the stakes of the decision concrete. Doom scenes are vivid
but not gruesome. Endings are three-part (verdict, aftermath, last page).

## Pipeline
`gen-salt.mjs outline` (one Flash-Lite call per region segment -> scene seeds) -> `prose` (batches of ~10 in story order, resumable,
validated, one re-ask) -> `merge` (book.json) -> `lint-salt.mjs`. Every drafted section is listed in `content/pyoa/flashlite-sections.json`
under `books.saltglass`. Driver pauses at 11:55 (London) on 28 Oct 2026 until `content/pyoa/PRO-PASS-DONE` or `PRO-PASS-RELEASE` exists.
