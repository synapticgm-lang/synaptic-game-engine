TITLE: The Unwritten World
TEXT: Silence follows the fire, broken only by the soft, settling hiss of cooling metal and the occasional flutter of a half-burnt leaf drifting from the high ceiling. The Underlibrary is vast, dark, and utterly empty, stripped of every catalogue, register, and ink-stained marker that once gave it shape. You stand alone amid miles of blackened iron shelving that stretch into a gloom untouched by any lamp. The city above sleeps on, entirely unaware that its past has been reduced to soot and its future left entirely blank. There are no records to consult, no rules to break, and no index to tell you who you are or what came before. You drop your dry wooden stamp onto the ash-strewn floor; it makes no sound at all as it hits the stone. The Long Closing has arrived not with a locked door, but with the quiet peace of total oblivion, and you turn your back on the empty stacks to walk out into the dark.

Whole centuries of famine and plague are crossed out in bold strokes, replaced by neat, elegant paragraphs detailing prosperous years that never occurred. TITLE: The Final Volume
TEXT: The massive iron covers of the Index snap shut with a dull, final thud that echoes down every corridor of the endless stacks. The whispering margins fall silent at last, and the drifting ghosts settle like grey dust upon the stone, anchored to the floor by the permanence of the newly written truth. You stand at the Returns Desk with your lamp guttering low in its brass housing, watching the ink dry upon a world that has been entirely re-edited overnight. The city above will wake to a past it never lived and a future it cannot alter, all bound neatly between leather covers by an unseen hand. There are no books left to shelve, no readers waiting in the galleried halls, and no further entries to be logged in the ledgers. You blow out your lamp, leaving the Underlibrary to its dark, perfect order, and close the book on a silence that will last for a generation.
